4. THE BAR BEQUEST (C15 killed; its evidence carried into R7)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

   percent over ~5 Gyr (Fragkoudi et al. 2021 class).
   Observed: fast bars dominate (R_CR/R_bar < 1.4 in most measured
   galaxies) — a published, growing tension with the settlement.
   The deletion (no dark sink): minimal braking — bars stay fast.
   -> the field's own published tension is response-side evidence:
   the dark sector's braking response to bars is measurably WEAKER
   than the settlement's simplest face predicts. The killed move's
   corpse feeds the surviving registration (the series' bequest rule).
