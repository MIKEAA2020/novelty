## PART 2 — EVIDENCE LINES

### T1 · The epicycle tradition (Ptolemaic astronomy)

- **P1 (F):** From Apollonius (3rd c. BCE) through Ptolemy's *Almagest* (c. 150 CE) to Copernicus's *De revolutionibus* (1543), the tradition met every anomaly by adding apparatus — epicycles on epicycles, Ptolemy's equant, Ibn al-Shatir's epicyclets (c. 1350) — and deleted nothing, the exact pattern of "ordinary science adds."
- **P2 (P):** Apollonius's epicycles showed each planet's retrograde wanderings to be compositions of uniform circular motions (the apparatus underlying *Almagest* Books IX–XII), unifying apparent irregularity with regular motion — but the deepest division, the Aristotelian celestial/terrestrial split, stood untouched as a joint of nature, so only the mathematics was unified.
- **P3 (F):** Every quantity in the *Almagest* was a per-body fitted parameter — the lunar theory of Books IV–V tuned to Hipparchus's and Babylonian eclipse data, the planetary equants of Book IX tuned planet by planet — and no dimensioned universal constant of the system ever existed to install.
- **P4 (P):** Ptolemy built the solar theory by taking over Hipparchus's parameters wholesale (*Almagest* III) and leaned on Babylonian eclipse cycles, and his tables — through the Handy Tables to the Alfonsine Tables of 1252 — predicted eclipses and positions for over a millennium; yet the eccentric/epicycle equivalence demonstrated in the *Almagest* (after Apollonius) meant the same data fit rival models, so the system was fitted rather than derived.
- **P5 (F):** The tradition abandoned Eudoxus's homocentric spheres (which failed on the planets' brightness variations) and absorbed Hipparchus's solar theory intact, incorporating its inheritance without ever demoting a successful predecessor to a derived limiting case or specifying where that predecessor must fail.
- **P6 (P):** The eccentric and epicyclic hypotheses were known-equivalent formalisms (Apollonius's theorem, presented in the *Almagest*, Books III and XII), so the same mathematics carried contested physical meaning for 1,400 years — Ptolemy's own *Planetary Hypotheses* supplied a physical reading, and Ibn al-Haytham's *Doubts on Ptolemy* (c. 1028) and the Maragha school (al-Tusi's couple, 13th c.) kept the question alive — but the dissociation remained a chronic defect, never resolved by a deeper reinterpretation.
- **P7 (P):** Ptolemy knowingly paid for predictive accuracy by adopting the equant (*Almagest* IX.5–6), compromising the uniform-circular-motion principle his own methodological statement professed (*Almagest* IX.2), a debt his successors collected for 1,400 years — Ibn al-Haytham attacked it (1028), and Copernicus built epicyclets precisely to restore uniformity (*Commentariolus*, c. 1510; *De revolutionibus*, 1543) — a real founder's cost, but a compromise rather than a surrender of a treasured commitment.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for over a millennium — the Alfonsine Tables (1252), the Maragha and Samarkand observatories (Ulugh Beg's zīj, c. 1437), the Tusi couple reappearing in Copernicus's mathematics, and the Peuerbach–Regiomontanus *Epitome of the Almagest* (1496) that Copernicus himself used — then degenerating by the sixteenth century, when the Gregorian reform of 1582 had to delete ten accumulated days and Copernicus was already denouncing the equant, each marginal gain in accuracy demanding more circles rather than fewer.
- **P9 (P):** Ptolemy closed the crisis of the planets' apparent irregularity (Plato's save-the-phenomena demand), but the deeper question — what the mathematical circles were in the physical heavens — was an inherited, deliberately bracketed gap (the *Almagest* keeps mathematical astronomy separate from Aristotelian physics, and the *Planetary Hypotheses* only papered over the joint), so the tradition never re-founded its subject on a self-generated deeper crisis.

### T2 · Germ theory of disease

- **P1 (S):** Between Pasteur's swan-neck flask memoir (1861) and the defeat of Pouchet (settled by 1864) on one flank and Koch's bacteriology (1876–84) on the other, the theory made spontaneous generation, Sydenham-style "epidemic constitutions," and foul-air etiology all inadmissible in mainstream medicine by 1900.
- **P2 (S):** The theory unified the brewer's vat with the surgical ward — Lister, citing Pasteur's 1861 work on aerial organisms, performed the first antiseptic operation on the boy James Greenlees (August 1865) and published the method in the *Lancet* in 1867 — and joined fermentation (Pasteur's Lille memoir of 1857), animal epizootics (Koch's anthrax, 1876), and human epidemics under one class of causes.
- **P3 (F):** No constant of nature issued from the program: its quantitative arm, from Farr's Registrar-General statistics (from 1837) to Koch's postulates (1882–90), produced classifications and procedural criteria, not a dimensioned invariant that re-scales a conceptual system.
- **P4 (F):** The theory was experiment-first — the old data pointing its way (Snow's 1855 cholera monograph, Semmelweis's 1847 puerperal-fever results) had been rejected or marginalized under miasma rather than forcing a derivation — and it could be established only by new observation and technique: Pasteur's flasks (1861), Koch's anthrax lifecycle in mice (1876), the tubercle bacillus (demonstrated March 24, 1882).
- **P5 (P):** Contagionism's successes were absorbed — quarantine had worked since Ragusa's thirty-day *trentino* of 1377 because specific agents transmit, and the sanitary program's sewers worked by reservoir removal — but miasma itself was refuted outright (Koch's comma bacillus, 1884; the unfiltered-Hamburg versus filtered-Altona cholera contrast of 1892) rather than demoted to a limiting case.
- **P6 (F):** The theory's decisive evidentiary surface was demonstration — stain, culture, inoculation — not formalism: there was no inherited symbolic structure to reinterpret, and nothing in its record parallels Lorentz-to-Einstein or Schrödinger-to-Born.
- **P7 (P):** Pasteur paid real coin — abandoning the chemical-catalysis theory of fermentation he had absorbed from his discipline, in the 1857 "Mémoire sur la fermentation appelée lactique," and enduring Liebig's public ridicule until Liebig's death in 1873 — but the program's other signature episodes (Koch's tuberculin catastrophe of 1890, the founders' institutional wars) were costs imposed on the theory, not surrenders of held commitments by its makers.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest was huge and unordered — the anthrax vaccine at Pouilly-le-Fort (1881), the rabies vaccine given to Joseph Meister (July 6, 1885), diphtheria antitoxin by von Behring and Kitasato (December 1890; the first Nobel Prize, 1901), filterable viruses (Ivanovsky 1892, Beijerinck 1898), Ross's mosquito–malaria cycle (1897–98; Nobel 1902), and ultimately penicillin (1928) — with no degenerative phase yet.
- **P9 (S):** The theory closed the half-century contagion–miasma deadlock that had paralyzed the International Sanitary Conferences since 1851 (resolved by Koch's comma bacillus, 1884, and the Hamburg–Altona contrast of 1892), and its own success opened deeper floors its successors still occupy — asymptomatic carriage (Mary Mallon, quarantined 1907, breaking the naive reading of Koch's postulates), immunology (Metchnikoff's phagocytes, 1883; Ehrlich's side-chain theory, 1897), and today's microbiome renegotiation of the pathogen concept itself.

### T3 · The luminiferous ether

- **P1 (F):** The ether was additive by construction: Huygens's *Traité de la Lumière* (lectured 1678, published 1690) posited a carrier medium, and every later generation piled on properties — Fresnel's transverse waves (1821) demanded a medium of near-zero density with shear rigidity, and Green's elasticity analysis (1838) multiplied the structure — until Einstein deleted the whole construct as "superfluous" in 1905.
- **P2 (S):** The program unified light with electromagnetism, domains thought separate, when Maxwell's "A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field" (1865) derived waves propagating at the Weber–Kohlrausch ratio (1856) and concluded that light consists in transverse undulations of "the same medium" that causes electric and magnetic phenomena.
- **P3 (F):** No universal constant was installed by the doctrine — the ether's elastic constants (Green 1838; MacCullagh's rotational ether) were indeterminate and adjusted between Fresnel's partial-dragging coefficient (1818, answering Arago's 1810 null) and Stokes's full entrainment (1845) — and the one great constant the program handled, c, was fixed by Maxwell's field equations and is credited by this instrument to 1905, not to the ether.
- **P4 (P):** The program had genuine derivation-first triumphs — Fresnel's 1819 prize memoir derived the bright spot at the center of a disc's shadow (Poisson's reductio, Arago's observation), and Maxwell's 1865 derivation of light's speed was honored by Hertz's 1887 waves — but the ether itself was hypothesis-first: its predicted drift failed (Michelson–Morley, 1887; Trouton–Noble, 1903) and was met with the ad hoc contractions of FitzGerald (1889) and Lorentz (1892).
- **P5 (P):** Fresnel's zone construction (1816–18) did embed ray optics as the short-wavelength limit of wave theory, conserving the predecessor's successes, but Newton's emission theory was defeated rather than derived as a limit, and the program's own core — the privileged rest frame — proved impossible for its successor to embed, since 1905 declared it superfluous rather than limiting.
- **P6 (S):** The ether is the canonical case of the equations surviving their meaning: Lorentz's "local time" (1895) and his 1904 transformations were auxiliary bookkeeping — he later conceded he had treated them as mathematical devices while retaining the ether (*The Theory of Electrons*, 1909) — and the profundity arrived only when Einstein, and Poincaré's June 1905 note on the Lorentz group, made the same mathematics say that time itself is relative.
- **P7 (F):** The founders defended rather than paid — FitzGerald (1889) and Lorentz (1892) met the 1887 null result with a contraction hypothesis, Lorentz still argued for retaining the ether after conceding relativity's economy (1909), Poincaré kept the ether in "L'espace et le temps" (1912), and Oliver Lodge defended it to his death (1940) — so the surrender of the ether, when it came, was made by an outsider (Einstein, 1905), not by the program's makers.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for a century — Fresnel's optics and polarization laws, Maxwell's equations vindicated by Hertz (1887) and Marconi's December 1901 transatlantic signal, Lorentz's electron theory explaining the Zeeman effect (shared Nobel, 1902), Michelson's interferometry founding precision metrology — then degenerating after 1887, as each null result (Rayleigh–Brace 1902, Trouton–Noble 1903) was purchased with another ad hoc patch (Lorentz 1895, 1904) that returned nothing.
- **P9 (S):** Fresnel's victory over the emission theory (the 1819 prize; the Poisson spot) closed the crisis of light's nature by opening a deeper one — what the medium was and how matter moved through it — which culminated in the Michelson–Morley null of 1887 and was closed by Einstein's 1905 relativity, built directly on the Maxwell–Lorentz floor the ether program had laid.

### T4 · Miasma theory of disease

- **P1 (P):** The doctrine's Hippocratic birth did delete something — *On the Sacred Disease* (c. 400 BCE) declared epilepsy no more divine than other maladies — but its mature nineteenth-century form only added and adapted, meeting Snow's 1855 waterborne cholera evidence with restatements such as Farr's elevation model (1852) and Pettenkofer's ground-water theory (1860s–70s).
- **P2 (P):** Chadwick's 1842 *Report on the Sanitary Condition of the Labouring Population* unified epidemic diseases into one filth-class, formalized in Farr's "zymotic" category in the vital-statistics returns — a real administrative unification, but one that merged diseases with different transmission routes (waterborne cholera, louse-borne typhus) under a single label rather than dissolving a joint in nature.
- **P3 (F):** Nothing constant issued from the doctrine — its quantitative arm, Farr's Registrar-General returns (from 1837) and the epidemic curves of 1848–66, was descriptive statistics whose fitted coefficients (the 1852 elevation gradient) shifted from epidemic to epidemic.
- **P4 (F):** The theory was the incumbent, and data broke it rather than being derived from it: Farr's atmospheric-elevation model could not account for the mortality cliff at the water-company boundary in Snow's 1853–54 Southwark–Lambeth comparison, which the miasmatist establishment refused to accept.
- **P5 (F):** The tradition refuted rather than embedded its rivals — *On the Sacred Disease* simply contradicted temple etiology, and the nineteenth-century sanitarians fought contagionist practice (the lazaretti and quarantines dating to 1377) to a standstill at the International Sanitary Conferences (1851 onward) instead of explaining why that practice worked and where it failed.
- **P6 (F):** There was no formalism for meaning to come apart from — the theory's vocabulary (miasma, effluvium, epidemic constitution) was verbal and its mathematics was borrowed vital statistics, leaving no symbolic surface on which interpretation could do deep work.
- **P7 (F):** The tradition's leaders famously refused to pay in their own coin: Chadwick (d. 1890) and Nightingale (d. 1910) remained miasmatist-sanitarians after Koch's comma bacillus (1884), and Pettenkofer dramatized his refusal by drinking a cholera culture in 1892 to refute Koch's specific-agent claim, surviving the dose without conceding the point.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for a phase — Chadwick's 1842 Report, the 1848 Public Health Act, Bazalgette's London interceptor sewers (from 1859), and London's last great cholera epidemic of 1866 striking where sewage still reached drinking water — then degenerating after 1884, when the returns went to filtration and germ-based measures while the theory's defenders paid in ad hoc restatements (Pettenkofer's 1892 self-experiment).
- **P9 (F):** The deadlock the doctrine inhabited was closed by bacteriology (Koch 1884; Hamburg–Altona 1892), and what survived of it — environmental health, the social gradient of disease — was absorbed as practice rather than handed on as a deeper self-generated crisis, so the doctrine left no open floor of its own making.

### T5 · Newtonian mechanics

- **P1 (S):** The *Principia* (1687) deleted the two-worlds cosmos — Book III's moon test (Prop. 4) put the falling apple and the orbiting Moon under one mechanics — and Book II's vortex propositions (Section IX, Props. 51–53) made Descartes's celestial carrying-medium unusable in serious physics thereafter.
- **P2 (S):** The same move unified them: terrestrial gravity attenuated by the inverse square suffices to hold the Moon (Book III, Prop. 4), while Book I derived Kepler's area and ellipse laws from the laws of motion (Props. 1–2, 11) and the *System of the World*'s mountain-cannon image joined Galileo's parabolic projectiles to planetary ellipses as one orbit family.
- **P3 (S):** Universal gravitation installed G in 1687 as the constant coupling matter to matter, and the constant became measurable fact with Cavendish's torsion balance, "Experiments to Determine the Density of the Earth" (1798).
- **P4 (S):** Newton needed almost no new observation — Richer's 1672 Cayenne pendulum result sufficed for the oblatity argument — and the promissory notes were honored across a century and a half: Halley's comet (predicted 1705, observed by Palitzsch on December 25, 1758), the Lapland arc (Maupertuis, 1736), and Neptune (found by Galle, September 23, 1846, within a degree of Le Verrier's computation, 159 years after the *Principia*).
- **P5 (S):** The *Principia* derived its predecessors and located their failures — Book I derives Kepler from the laws of motion, Book III shows where Kepler must fail (Jupiter–Saturn perturbations, the lunar inequalities, later closed within Newtonian methods by Clairaut's 1749 apsidal calculation), and Galileo's parabolic trajectories become the local limit of orbital motion.
- **P6 (P):** Newton's profundity was new formalism and new meaning fused rather than an interpretation of inherited mathematics; yet the formalism then outran its meaning — the General Scholium's "hypotheses non fingo" (1713) left attraction without mechanism, and the meaning of the same mechanics was contested from the Leibniz–Clarke correspondence (1715–16) over absolute space to Mach's 1883 reinterpretation of the bucket argument, which later fed Einstein.
- **P7 (S):** Newton knowingly surrendered the mechanical philosophy's ban on action at a distance, telling Bentley (1692–93) that gravity "innate, inherent, and essential to matter" is "so great an absurdity" that no competent thinker could accept it, and paid in the attacks of Huygens (*Discours de la cause de la pesanteur*, 1690, accepting the inverse square but not the attraction) and Leibniz's "occult quality" charges (1715–16).
- **P8 (S):** The un-ordered harvest ran for two centuries — Halley's comet 1758, the oblate Earth after 1736, Laplace's *Mécanique céleste* (1799–1825) and solar-system stability, Neptune 1846, and Poincaré's 1890 three-body prize essay growing into chaos theory — and even its one expensive failure, the Vulcan searches after Le Verrier's 1859 Mercury anomaly (1859–1909), was outweighed by the returns classical mechanics kept making across physics.
- **P9 (S):** Newton closed the Kepler–Galileo crises while leaving the mechanism of gravity explicitly open ("hypotheses non fingo," 1713) — a wound occupied productively for 228 years by celestial mechanics until the field equations of November 25, 1915 closed it by identifying gravity with geometry.

### T6 · Phlogiston chemistry

- **P1 (F):** Stahl's theory renamed rather than deleted — Becher's "terra pinguis" (*Physica Subterranea*, 1669) became phlogiston (1703) — and its whole career was immune addition: Cavendish's "inflammable air" (1766) was absorbed as phlogiston itself, and the calcination weight-gain was absorbed by assigning phlogiston levity rather than surrendering anything.
- **P2 (P):** The theory genuinely unified combustion, calcination, and smelting as one reversible process-class — the calx–metal pair with charcoal's phlogiston transferring in the reduction — but the unity was a posited substance rather than the dissolution of a descriptive artifact, and the true unification of the same phenomena was Lavoisier's (1777–89), achieved by deleting phlogiston.
- **P3 (F):** No invariant issued from the doctrine: the quantified weight gain on calcination (Jean Rey 1630; Guyton de Morveau's confirmations of 1772) was met with the "levity" of phlogiston, a retro-fit that fixed no constant and re-scaled nothing.
- **P4 (F):** The theory was a qualitative frame under-constrained by data — it issued no risky numerical promissory note, and where quantity pressed (from Rey 1630 to Guyton de Morveau 1772 on calcination weights), the theory was adjusted to the data rather than deriving it.
- **P5 (F):** Stahl incorporated Becher's scheme wholesale rather than deriving it as a limit or marking where it fails, and the theory itself could not be embedded by its successor in any limit: there is no regime in which combustion is a release, and Lavoisier's *Traité élémentaire de chimie* (1789) re-founded chemistry keeping the gases and the balance but discarding the concept.
- **P6 (F):** There was no formalism for meaning to come apart from — the theory's most formal artifacts, Geoffroy's affinity tables (1718) and Bergman's 1775 elaborations, never became mathematics, so no reinterpretation of inherited symbolism could carry its depth.
- **P7 (F):** The theory cost its makers nothing they held — Stahl inherited Becher's frame intact — and its late defenders illustrate the refusal of payment: Priestley published against Lavoisier to the end (*Considerations on the Doctrine of Phlogiston*, 1796; he died in 1804 unconvinced), and Cavendish read his own 1784 water-synthesis experiments in phlogistic terms rather than concede.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for decades — the theory organized pneumatic chemistry and delivered its materials: Black's fixed air (1754–56), Cavendish's inflammable air (1766), Priestley's dephlogisticated air (1774–75), Scheele's fire air and "dephlogisticated marine acid" (1774) — then degenerating after the 1783–85 water controversy, when Kirwan's *Essay on Phlogiston* (1789), translated by Marie-Anne Lavoisier with the French chemists' annexed rebuttals, marked the point at which each defense (negative weight, hydrogen-as-phlogiston) cost more than it returned.
- **P9 (P):** The theory organized its domain and left open the weight-anomaly crack through which quantitative chemistry entered — but the questions it bequeathed (what the airs were; what the balance-sheet meant) sat at the same depth as those it answered, and its successors built on Lavoisier's and Dalton's floors rather than on any floor phlogiston itself laid.

### T7 · Quantum mechanics

- **P1 (S):** The quantum deleted determinism and the visualizable trajectory at once — Planck's discontinuous energy elements (December 14, 1900), Born's probabilistic reading of ψ (June 1926), and Heisenberg's indeterminacy paper (received March 23, 1927, "On the Intuitive Content...") made simultaneous exact position-and-momentum unthinkable in physics thereafter.
- **P2 (S):** The theory unified wave and particle, and matter with light — de Broglie's 1924 thesis extended duality to matter, confirmed by Davisson–Germer and G. P. Thomson's electron diffraction (both 1927) — and it unified chemistry with physics when the Pauli exclusion principle (January 1925) derived the periodic table and Heitler–London (1927) derived the covalent bond.
- **P3 (S):** Planck's h, introduced December 14, 1900 and fixed against the blackbody measurements of Lummer–Pringsheim and Rubens–Kurlbaum (1899–1900), discretizes action and re-scales every domain it enters, from Bohr's 1913 atom to the transistor.
- **P4 (P):** The record splits by birthday: the 1900 origin was interpolation to measurements weeks old (Rubens–Kurlbaum, reported to Planck in February 1900), while the 1925 consolidation was derivation from decades-old data — Heisenberg's *Umdeutung* paper (July 1925) rebuilt mechanics from the Balmer series (1885) and the Ritz combination rules (1908) — with promissory notes honored fast (electron diffraction, 1927; Dirac's 1928 equation anticipating Anderson's positron, 1932).
- **P5 (S):** Quantum mechanics embeds its predecessor as a limit — Bohr's correspondence principle (the 1913 atom; the 1918 "On the Quantum Theory of Line-Spectra") built quantum corrections onto classical frequencies, Ehrenfest's 1927 theorem carried Newton's equations into wave mechanics as expectation values, and the WKB limit (1926) recovers classical trajectories as h vanishes.
- **P6 (S):** The theory is the definition's second exemplar: Schrödinger published his equation in 1926 intending a wave ontology, and Born's June 1926 collision papers (with the famous footnote correcting ψ to |ψ|²) made the same mathematics say probability — after which Bohr's Como lecture (September 1927) fixed the complementarity reading over Schrödinger's protests.
- **P7 (S):** The founders paid in their own coin — Planck confessed the quantum hypothesis was "an act of desperation" (letter to R. W. Wood, October 7, 1931), Schrödinger protested the probabilistic reading during his 1926 Copenhagen visit and staged the cat paradox (1935) against it, and Einstein, the theory's midwife, resisted its indeterminism from the 1927 Solvay debates to EPR (1935) for the rest of his life.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest continues after a century with no degenerative phase — the transistor (Bardeen–Brattain, December 1947), the laser (Schawlow–Townes 1958; Maiman 1960), superconductivity explained (BCS 1957), and quantum information from Bell's 1964 theorem through Aspect (1982) to the 2022 Nobel Prize.
- **P9 (S):** The theory closed the atomic-spectra crisis (Balmer, the Bohr atom) and opened the measurement problem, which has stayed open and productive for a century — from the 1927 Solvay debates and EPR (1935) through Bell (1964) and Aspect (1982) to quantum information science, the wound became the floor on which a new discipline stands.

### T8 · Relativity

- **P1 (S):** The 1905 paper declared the ether "superfluous" and its §1 train analysis made absolute simultaneity unthinkable ("Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper," received June 30, 1905), and general relativity completed the deletion by dissolving gravitational force into geometry (the November 1915 field equations).
- **P2 (S):** Special relativity unified space with time (Einstein 1905; Minkowski's "Raum und Zeit," 1908) and mass with energy ("Ist die Trägheit eines Körpers von seinem Energieinhalt abhängig?," September 1905), and general relativity unified gravity with geometry — the 1907 equivalence principle ("the happiest thought of my life") maturing into the field equations of November 25, 1915.
- **P3 (S):** The light postulate of 1905 installs c as the invariant speed of influence — turning Maxwell's Weber–Kohlrausch-derived value (1856/1865) from a property of a medium into a law of kinematics — and general relativity later added its second constant, Λ (1917), revived by the 1998 accelerating-universe observations.
- **P4 (S):** Special relativity was over-determined by an eighteen-year-old null result (Michelson–Morley, 1887) and used no new data; general relativity consumed a fifty-six-year-old anomaly, computing Mercury's 43″ per century on November 18, 1915 (Le Verrier's problem of 1859) — and the promissory notes were honored on schedule: light bending at 1.75″ (Eddington's eclipse expedition, May 29, 1919), gravitational redshift (Adams's Sirius B, 1925; Pound–Rebka, 1959), and gravitational waves (LIGO's GW150914, September 14, 2015, ninety-nine years after the prediction).
- **P5 (S):** Relativity demotes rather than refutes — special relativity reduces to Galilean kinematics as v/c → 0 (the 1905 velocity-addition theorem), and Einstein explicitly demanded recovery of the Newtonian limit while finalizing the November 1915 equations, which is why Newtonian mechanics still flies spacecraft.
- **P6 (S):** The theory is the definition's first exemplar: Lorentz (1895, 1904) and Poincaré (the June 1905 *Comptes rendus* note naming the Lorentz group) held the transformations as devices tied to an ether frame — Lorentz later conceded he had treated local time as auxiliary while defending the ether (*The Theory of Electrons*, 1909) — and Einstein's reading made the same mathematics say that time itself is relative.
- **P7 (S):** Einstein paid twice in his own coin — he surrendered the ether presupposed by his own 1895 Aarau school essay "On the Investigation of the State of the Ether in a Magnetic Field," then, when the quantum asked him for determinism, declined and spent thirty years resisting, from the 1927 Solvay debates through EPR (1935) to his 1949 Schilpp reply.
- **P8 (S):** The century's harvest was un-ordered and continues — modern cosmology (Friedmann 1922, Lemaître 1927, Hubble 1929, the CMB after 1965), black holes (Schwarzschild's January 1916 solution through Oppenheimer–Snyder 1939, Cygnus X-1 in 1971, the Event Horizon Telescope image of 2019), gravitational-wave astronomy (LIGO 2015), and the relativistic corrections that keep GPS (from 1978) accurate.
- **P9 (S):** General relativity closed Newton's 228-year wound — the mechanism of gravity — on November 25, 1915, and immediately opened the deeper crisis that defines fundamental physics today: the incompatibility of dynamical spacetime with quantum theory, sharpened to the black-hole information paradox (Hawking 1974–76; the 2012 firewall debates).
