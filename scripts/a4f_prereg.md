## Locked coding — THE FIXITY OF SPECIES / NATURAL THEOLOGY (dead side)

P1 Deletion at birth: **F** — the doctrine deleted nothing at its
installation. In its consolidated form (the form the multi-century
convention codes: natural theology's fixism from Ray and Linnaeus to
1859) it was the inherited default, not a deletion: Aristotle's
essentialism carried forward, renamed and tightened by the natural-
theology settlement. The transformation alternative stayed thinkable
the entire time — Anaximander's zoogony and Lucretius's DRN V in
antiquity; Buffon's masked degeneration hints (1766) inside the
orthodoxy itself; Lamarck publishing transformism (1800-1809) and
being answered in the field's own journals. Birth-window: within the
window, no rival question died.

P2 Unification: **P** — vocabulary-level: Linnaeus's natural system
gave one ordering to all life (the search for the "natural system"),
and the oeconomia naturae (Linnaeus's own essays, 1749) bound species
into one designed economy. But no law of nature was unified and no
deep ancient distinction dissolved: the distinction the doctrine
needed — species as kinds rather than populations — was asserted, not
dissolved, and the artificial-versus-natural classification question
stayed open inside the program for a century. Law-vs-vocabulary
amendment: vocabulary unification only.

P3 Universal constant: **F** — nothing. The doctrine was
pre-quantitative: no number installed, none re-scaled anything, and
the one candidate ordering (the Scala Naturae) was a hierarchy, not a
measure. The domain-marked discriminator predicts F here as it did for
miasma and phlogiston.

P4 Derivation-first (structure-not-content): **F** — the design
inference was retrodictive by nature: each adaptation read as designed
after the fact, each new fossil accommodated by a new special creation
or a local catastrophe — content retrofits without limit, and the
doctrine issued no promissory note that was ever honored. No structure
survival either: unlike caloric's Carnot theorem, the fixist program
produced no theorem the successor kept.

P5 Conservative embedding: **P** (two faces, direction F) — predecessor
retention: maximal (Aristotle's essences, the Chain of Being, the whole
inherited apparatus retained, which the A4b amendment prices as
cheap); apparatus survival: real and total — the taxonomy, the museums,
the type specimens, the fossil archive all survived INTO the successor
(archive survival, not theorem survival); successor-recovery: F — the
doctrine recovered no displaced theory as a limit and stated no failure
condition anywhere. The machinery that survived was archival, not
mathematical — the exact inverse of caloric's Fourier-equation
survival, printed as the pair's asymmetry.

P6 Formalism/meaning (operationalized: minted measurables): **F** — no
formalism and no measurable minted: the species concept itself resisted
quantification (the "species problem" was born inside the doctrine and
inherited by the successor); no unit, no instrument demanded by the
theory, no discriminating list that could return a negative result.
Fails as written and fails operationalized — parity with phlogiston's
and miasma's cells, below caloric's clean partial.

P7 Founders' payment-not-defense: **F** — installation cost its makers
nothing. Linnaeus, Cuvier, and Paley held the ambient institutional
view; no founder surrendered a cherished commitment to install fixism.
The documented costs run the other way — Cuvier's campaigns against
Lamarck and Geoffroy were defense of the doctrine at the price of
others, not payment for it. Defense is not payment (A4b doctrine).

P8 Generative slope: **S then D** — generative phase: the natural-

P9 Crisis-chaining: **P** — the program's own instruments minted the
questions its executioner answered: why the fossil succession, why

system — the questions were raised inside the program (Lyell's
