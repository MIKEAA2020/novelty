The L3 Protocol
Eight phases, eight gates — the production pipeline for deep novelty
P0 · CRISIS CARTOGRAPHY
find the strain
ledger of accurate-but-unspeakable data
▸ artifact: the anomaly ledger
G0 ▸ unspeakable anomaly · patch-thickness ≥ 3
KILL ↩ all patchable → downgrade to L2
P1 · CONCEPT AUTOPSY
find what to delete
inherited concepts isolated by census
▸ artifact: dependency graph + census
G1 ▸ inherited concept of maximal weight
KILL ↩ all tested → P0
P2 · AMPUTATION
delete, and survive
meaningless, not false — or promote it
▸ artifact: amputated axioms + derivations
G2 ▸ core preserved, new structure forced
KILL ↩ decorative or fatal → P1
P3 · DICTIONARY ATTACK
mine the untranslatable
translate two domains, mint at residue
▸ artifact: dictionary + residue list
G3 ▸ residue non-empty · compression positive
KILL ↩ perfect translation = reformulation
P4 · INVARIANCE & CONSTANT
write law at bedrock
state what stays unchanged; price it
▸ artifact: invariance statement + dimensions
G4 ▸ one-sentence invariance + constant
KILL ↩ a constant fitted to data is a patch
P5 · LIMIT-CASE INVERSION
pay the old theory
derive it as a computed limit
▸ artifact: derivation + discriminating list
G5 ▸ predecessor computed as limit
KILL ↩ no limit / no measurable difference
P6 · SEMANTIC ASSIGNMENT
make it mean a world
a measurement story for every primitive
▸ artifact: the operational dictionary
G6 ▸ every primitive measurable in principle
KILL ↩ leans on deleted concept → P2
P7 · ADVERSARIAL CLOSURE
resist it, then enumerate
defend the old order; name the next crisis
▸ artifact: defense + harvest + crisis
G7 ▸ defense answered · harvest ≥ 10
KILL ↩ closure without opening = terminal
G7 PASSED — L3 CANDIDATE · ratification belongs to the world
LOOP RULE  A KILL returns control to its return-target; three kills at the same gate abandon the target and demand a post-mortem. The kill log is the operator’s compounding asset — every entry maps where the bedrock is not.
