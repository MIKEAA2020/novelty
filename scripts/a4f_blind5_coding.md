## PART 2 — EVIDENCE LINES

## T1 · The caloric theory of heat

- **P1 (F):** The founding act was additive — Lavoisier's *Traité élémentaire de chimie* (1789) simply added "calorique" to its table of simple substances while expressly declining to determine its nature, and the deep deletions of 1789 (of phlogiston) belonged to the oxygen-combustion theory outside this scope.
- **P2 (P):** Caloric gave one substance-account of Black's latent heat (c. 1760) and of expansion (via its self-repulsion), and Carnot's *Réflexions* (1824) treated heat's fall from hot to cold as literally a waterfall — a unification of the thermal phenomena that nonetheless *hardened* the heat/mechanics joint into an ontological difference rather than dissolving it.
- **P3 (F):** No constant of G/c/h rank was installed: Carnot's 1824 analysis fixed only the efficiency bound as a function of the two reservoir temperatures, and the tradition left its medium's physical constants forever undetermined (Fourier's 1822 *Théorie analytique de la chaleur* reduced conductivity to an empirical coefficient).
- **P4 (F):** The theory was contradicted by old data rather than over-determined by it — Rumford's Munich cannon-boring experiments (1798) and Davy's ice-rubbing (1799) produced heat without caloric expenditure — and no promissory note was later honored in its own terms (the searches for the weight of caloric came back null).
- **P5 (F):** No successful predecessor was demoted: Black's calorimetry was absorbed as bookkeeping rather than derived, the friction results of Rumford and Davy were patched rather than predicted, and when the theory fell (Clausius, 1850) it was refuted outright rather than embedded.
- **P6 (F):** The formalism/meaning split ran against the theory — Carnot's 1824 cycle mathematics was preserved verbatim in Clapeyron's 1834 analytic reworking and in Thomson's 1848 absolute-scale paper while its caloric meaning was discarded, so the theory supplied the discarded reading, not the reinterpretation.
- **P7 (F):** No founder paid a visible coin: Lavoisier hedged in the *Traité*, Regnault measured without ontological commitment, and the one founder who lost faith — Carnot, whose posthumous notebooks (published 1878) abandoned caloric for heat-as-motion with a mechanical-equivalent estimate — did so privately, publishing nothing before his death in 1832.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative through Fourier's 1822 heat equation, Carnot's 1824 theorem, Clapeyron's 1834 analytics, and Regnault's steam measurements (which fed Kelvin's 1848 absolute temperature scale), then degenerating through the 1840s as the program paid more for each defense of caloric conservation against Joule's 1843-49 mechanical-equivalent experiments than any result returned, until Clausius dissolved it in 1850.
- **P9 (P):** It closed Black's calorimetric-accounting problem and, through Carnot's 1824 analysis, opened the heat-work question that became the central crisis of the 1840s — but it handed that crisis over as an unresolved contradiction (Carnot's conserved caloric against Joule's conversion) rather than as a floor of its own construction.

## T2 · Darwinian evolution by natural selection

- **P1 (S):** The *Origin* (1859) made the independent creation and immutability of species scientifically untenable ("extinction and natural selection go hand in hand"), and the *Descent of Man* (1871) then deleted the human/animal boundary on which Ray's *Wisdom of God* (1691) and Paley's *Natural Theology* (1802) had been built.
- **P2 (S):** Darwin unified artificial and natural selection through the pigeon-fanciers of *Origin* chapter 1, dissolved the species/variety distinction into genealogical flow ("our classifications will come to be, as far as they can be so made, genealogies," *Origin* ch. 14), and made man and beast one tree.
- **P3 (F):** No universal constant entered: the theory's core quantities — variation, heredity, selective pressure — were not measurable in Darwin's apparatus, and nothing playing the role of G, c, or h appeared between 1859 and 1871.
- **P4 (S):** The theory was over-determined by old data already in Darwin's hands — Malthus's *Essay* (1798, read October 1838), Lyell's *Principles of Geology* (1830-33), the Beagle collections (1831-36), and the breeders' records — with no new instruments; and its promissory notes were honored: the predicted pollinator of *Angraecum sesquipedale* (*Fertilisation of Orchids*, 1862) was found as *Xanthopan morgani praedicta* in Madagascar (Rothschild and Jordan, 1903), and *Archaeopteryx* surfaced at Solnhofen in 1861, two years after publication.
- **P5 (P):** Darwin explained why the old view *worked* — the appearance of design from cumulative selection (*Origin*, chs. 4-6) and the apparent fixity of species across human timescales — but special creation was refuted rather than demoted to a formal limiting case, so the embedding remains qualitative.
- **P6 (P):** The reinterpretive structure is real — Owen's homologies and archetype (*On the Nature of Limbs*, 1849) were made to mean common ancestry, and older classificatory "trees" (e.g., Augier's *Élémens de botanique*, 1801) became literal genealogy in Darwin's 1837 "I think" notebook sketch — but the theory operated on descriptive apparatus rather than mathematical formalism, so it only partially instantiates the point.
- **P7 (S):** Darwin paid in his own coin: a Cambridge man prepared for Anglican orders who wrote in his *Autobiography* that he hardly ever admired a book more than Paley's *Natural Theology*, he surrendered that worldview (Emma Darwin's letters on faith; the twenty-year delay 1838-1858), and his May 1860 letter to Asa Gray on the ichneumon wasps records the cost of explaining cruelty without design.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest saturated even through the later "eclipse of Darwinism" (Julian Huxley's phrase): *Archaeopteryx* (1861), Bates's Amazonian mimicry (1862), Fritz Müller's 1878 mathematical account of mimicry, biogeography, and paleoanthropology — a program still generating.
- **P9 (S):** Darwin closed the species/adaptation problem by opening the deeper one of heredity: his own pangenesis theory (*Variation*, 1868) failed, Mendel's 1866 paper went unread until 1900, and the mechanism of inheritance remained biology's open wound for half a century.

## T3 · The epicycle tradition (Ptolemaic astronomy)

- **P1 (F):** The tradition's signature was addition — anomaly was met with another epicycle, and its most original device, the equant of the *Almagest* (2nd century CE), was added machinery to save the phenomena — while the superlunary/sublunary joint of Aristotelian cosmology was preserved untouched.
- **P2 (P):** One mathematical toolkit did unify the five planets, Sun, and Moon under common procedures (the Maragha school extending it, al-Tusi's couple appearing c. 1259), but the tradition maintained rather than dissolved the deepest distinction, between the heavens and the Earth.
- **P3 (F):** No universal constant: the tradition's numbers were per-planet fitted parameters — periods from Babylonian records, solar and lunar elements from Hipparchus — and even its precession rate (Ptolemy's 1 degree per century, adopted from Hipparchus) was a datum later corrected by al-Battani (c. 900), not a constant of the system.
- **P4 (F):** The method was parameter-fitting to accumulated observations — Ptolemy's lunar theory (*Almagest*, Books IV-V) adjusts Hipparchus's parameters to Babylonian eclipse cycles — and its tables, down to the Alfonsine Tables (1252), interpolated the same record rather than issuing promissory notes of a new kind.
- **P5 (F):** No predecessor was embedded with failure conditions: Ptolemy absorbed Hipparchus's solar and lunar theory wholesale, Eudoxus's homocentric spheres were abandoned rather than derived as limits, and the tradition's response to its own anomalies was added devices (Ibn al-Shatir's c. 1350 reforms *replaced*, rather than derived, Ptolemy's models while keeping the appearances).
- **P6 (P):** The formalism/meaning split is genuine in the record — Apollonius's equivalence of eccentric and epicyclic hypotheses is demonstrated in *Almagest* III, Ibn al-Haytham's *Al-Shukuk* (c. 1030) attacked the equant's violation of uniform circular motion, and Copernicus (1543) re-used the very devices (including Ibn al-Shatir's models, per Swerdlow and Neugebauer) to say heliocentrism — but the tradition treated the split as a defect to patch, not as its profundity.
- **P7 (F):** No founder-figure paid a coin: Ptolemy offered his models with mathematical pragmatism (deferring physical meaning to the *Planetary Hypotheses*), Hipparchus left no confession of cost, and the tradition's doubts (Ibn al-Haytham again) were paid by its critics rather than its founders.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for well over a millennium — it produced trigonometry (Ptolemy's chord table), the *zij* tradition, the Alfonsine Tables (1252), and Ulugh Beg's Samarkand catalogue (1430s) — then degenerating by Tycho's era, when the 1572 nova and the 1577 comet forced repairs and each successive table (the Prutenic of 1551, then Tycho's own program) bought accuracy at rising conceptual cost.
- **P9 (P):** It closed the retrograde-motion problem and left successors a working floor (Ptolemy's star catalogue and parameters were still Tycho's comparanda), but the deeper question it opened — whether the models were physically real — it left as an unhealed wound from Ibn al-Haytham's *Doubts* to Osiander's anonymous preface to *De revolutionibus* (1543), not as a re-founded subject.

## T4 · The fixity of species (natural theology)

- **P1 (F):** The doctrine was additive and conservative — Ray's *Wisdom of God* (1691), Paley's *Natural Theology* (1802), and the eight Bridgewater Treatises (1833-40) accumulated design arguments — and it deleted nothing, since transmutation had never been a live scientific option for its makers.
- **P2 (P):** The design argument did unify all adaptations under one cause (Paley's watch), and Linnaeus's *Systema Naturae* (1735) gave one cataloguing system for all of nature, but the unification was by an external artificer, and the theory hardened rather than dissolved the species-as-essence distinction.
- **P3 (F):** No constant: the doctrine's quantities were typological counts — Linnaeus's genera and species, Cuvier's four embranchements (1812) — with nothing playing the role of G, c, or h.
- **P4 (F):** Its arguments were inductive compilations issuing no novel predictions — Paley's watch (1802) and the Bridgewater Treatises organized known adaptations — and even Cuvier's palaeontology (the 1796 mammoth paper; the *Ossements fossiles* of 1812) found only repeated catastrophes requiring new creations no one could foretell.
- **P5 (F):** The tradition absorbed anomalies by multiplication rather than by embedding: extinction, established in Cuvier's 1796 paper on the mammoth and mastodon, was accommodated by successive catastrophes and restocking creations rather than by deriving any predecessor's limits.
- **P6 (F):** The doctrine had no formalism whose meaning could come apart — its content was descriptive and theological, so the Lorentz-before-Einstein structure cannot arise.
- **P7 (P):** The one founder's coin visible in the record is Linnaeus's late concession — after examining the peloric toadflax (1744) he allowed in later editions of *Species Plantarum* (1762-63) that some species arise by hybridization within created genera — a real if small surrender of the fixity his own *Systema* had proclaimed.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative as the sponsor of natural history — Ray's *Historia Plantarum* (1686-1704), the global cataloguing by Linnaeus's students, Cuvier's founding of comparative anatomy and palaeontology (1796-1812) — then degenerating after 1859, when the program's explanatory core could only fund defenses (Mivart's *Genesis of Species*, 1871) while its descriptive harvest passed to the evolutionists.
- **P9 (F):** The doctrine closed questions by referring them to the Creator and opened no deeper floor: its end (the Oxford confrontation of June 1860 and after) was a defensive collapse, not a re-founding that handed successors a deeper problem.

## T5 · Germ theory of disease

- **P1 (S):** The theory made its rivals unthinkable: Pasteur's swan-neck flask experiments (*Mémoire sur les corpuscules organisés*, 1861) destroyed spontaneous generation, and after Koch's isolations — anthrax (1876), the tubercle bacillus (March 1882), the cholera vibrio (1883-84) — explanations of epidemics by effluvia and "epidemic constitutions" lost scientific standing.
- **P2 (S):** It unified domains treated as separate — fermentation (Pasteur's lactic-acid memoir of 1857), silkworm disease (the pébrine campaign in the Midi, 1865-70), veterinary and human anthrax (Koch, 1876), human tuberculosis (1882) — revealing that the boundaries between chemistry, agriculture, and medicine were artifacts: one class of microscopic beings ferments, rots, and infects.
- **P3 (F):** No universal constant: the theory's entities were named species (*Bacillus anthracis*, *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*, *Vibrio cholerae*) and its methods (Koch's postulates, 1884) installed criteria, not constants of G/c/h rank.
- **P4 (P):** The theory was over-determined by old clinical observations — Bassi's muscardine work (1835), Snow's Broad Street investigation (1854), Semmelweis's Vienna clinics (1847) — and its promissory notes were honored (Manson's mosquito-malaria conjecture confirmed by Ross's Secunderabad dissections, 1897; Finlay's 1881 mosquito theory of yellow fever confirmed by the Reed commission, 1900); but its acceptance required Koch's new laboratory technology of solid culture media and staining (1881), so it was not purely derivation-first.
- **P5 (P):** Germ theory conserved the sanitary program's *practice* — explaining why sewerage and clean water worked (Bazalgette's London main drainage, 1859-75) and why miasma failed (the 1892 Hamburg epidemic versus filtered-water Altona) — and even retained a partial "bad air" mechanism in TB's airborne transmission; but miasma was refuted rather than formally embedded as a limiting case.
- **P6 (P):** Meaning caught up to held practice: Davaine had seen the anthrax bacilli in blood (1850-63) without causal reading until Koch's pure-culture infections (1876), and Jenner's 1796 vaccination waited eighty years for Pasteur's attenuation principle (fowl cholera, 1880; the Pouilly-le-Fort anthrax trial, 1881; rabies, 1885) to be made to mean weakened living agents — the reinterpretation pattern, but without mathematical formalism.
- **P7 (P):** The founders' payments are real but partial: Koch paid publicly when tuberculin, announced as a cure at the 1890 Berlin congress, failed as therapy, and he resisted his own postulates' logic at the 1901 London congress by denying that bovine tuberculosis could infect humans — a founder's refusal later refuted.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest saturated: Behring's diphtheria antitoxin (1890, first Nobel Prize in Medicine, 1901), Lister's antiseptic surgery (1865 onward), Pasteur's rabies vaccine (1885), Fleming's penicillin from a contaminated plate (1928), and late harvests such as *H. pylori* and ulcers (Marshall and Warren, 1984; Nobel 2005) — still generating.
- **P9 (S):** It closed the two-thousand-year contagion/anticontagion dispute and re-founded etiology on Koch's postulates — then opened deeper floors immediately: the carrier state (Mary Mallon, "Typhoid Mary," identified 1907) and the mechanisms of host resistance, which founded immunology (Metchnikoff's phagocytes, 1883; Ehrlich's side-chains, 1897) and, past Chamberland's filters, virology (Ivanovsky, 1892; Beijerinck, 1898).

## T6 · The luminiferous ether

- **P1 (P):** The wave-ether program did delete a reigning rival — Newton's emission theory fell with Young's interference papers (1801-03) and Fresnel's diffraction memoir, the Poisson-Arago spot being verified during the 1819 Académie prize judging — but its own founding act was additive, postulating a plenum to save the mechanical ban on unmediated action, and the deletion did not hold (the photon returned in 1905).
- **P2 (S):** The tradition achieved real unifications: Fresnel's transverse waves (1821-22) brought polarization into the wave framework, and Maxwell's *A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field* (1865) made light an electromagnetic disturbance, revealing the light/electromagnetism distinction as an artifact of description.
- **P3 (F):** No constant of G/c/h rank was installed — c was inherited (Rømer 1676, Fizeau 1849, Foucault 1862) rather than installed by the ether, whose own physical constants (density, rigidity) were never determined, and whose signature parameter, Fresnel's dragging coefficient 1 - 1/n<super>2</super> (confirmed by Fizeau, 1851), was a corrective coefficient rather than a universal constant.
- **P4 (P):** The tradition contained a derivation-first core — Maxwell derived light's velocity from the electromagnetic constants (using Weber and Kohlrausch's 1856 ratio of units) and Hertz's 1887 spark experiments honored that promissory note twenty-two years later — but the ether hypothesis itself was under-determined, meeting the Michelson-Morley null (1887) with accommodation (FitzGerald's 1889 contraction conjecture, Lorentz's 1892 molecular-force derivation) rather than derivation.
- **P5 (S):** The wave program embedded its predecessor's results as a limiting case: rectilinear propagation, reflection, Snell's law, and even Newton's "fits of easy transmission" became the short-wavelength limit of wave optics, with the theory predicting where ray optics must fail — at edges and apertures, as in the Poisson spot (1818-19).
- **P6 (F):** The tradition was the scaffold rather than the agent: Lorentz's 1904 paper held the full Lorentz-covariant formalism while still meaning the ether, and the tradition's own dissociative gesture — Hertz's reduction of "Maxwell's theory" to "Maxwell's system of equations" (1890) — stripped meaning without supplying a new one; the reinterpretation was Einstein's (*Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper*, 1905).
- **P7 (P):** Maxwell paid a methodological coin in abandoning the idle-wheel vortex models of *On Physical Lines of Force* (1861-62) for the Lagrangian abstraction of 1865, and Lorentz retained the ether to his death (The Theory of Electrons, 1906/1915; he lived to 1928) after Einstein's deletion — the founder's refusal to surrender — but the founding generation of Huygens and Fresnel records no comparable payment.
- **P8 (S then D):** The harvest was enormous — Fresnel's polarization mathematics, Hertz's radio (1887), Lorentz's account of the Zeeman effect (Nobel with Zeeman, 1902), Drude's electron theory of metals (1900), and Michelson's interferometer (1881, 1887), whose descendants detected gravitational waves in 2015 — yet after 1887 the ether core paid more and more for each null result (the contractions of 1889/1892, the 1904 patchwork, the Kaufmann crisis of 1905-06) until 1905 declared the medium superfluous.
- **P9 (P):** It closed the propagation problem and left a productive open wound — the nature of the medium, from Huygens's *Traité* (1690) through Maxwell's abandoned mechanical models of 1861-62 — and it handed Einstein the Lorentz group as a floor; but the crisis it could not close, ether-drift detection, was closed against it, by declaring the ether superfluous.

## T7 · Miasma theory of disease

- **P1 (F):** The doctrine neither subtracted nor made anything unthinkable — it was the inherited default from the Hippocratic *Airs, Waters, Places*, and its nineteenth-century consolidation (Chadwick's 1842 *Report*; the Public Health Act of 1848) was institutional accumulation on top of an old frame.
- **P2 (P):** It did unify epidemic and endemic disease under one environmental cause — Farr even built a "zymotic" class of filth-diseases into the Registrar-General's nomenclature (1852) — but this was single-cause generalization rather than the dissolution of a joint, and the class itself dissolved under bacteriology (typhus louse-borne, cholera waterborne, yellow fever mosquito-borne).
- **P3 (F):** No constant was installed, and the doctrine's one quantitative product, Farr's elevation law of cholera mortality (1852), was a fitted regression that failed when water supply was controlled.
- **P4 (F):** The theory accommodated rather than derived: Snow's 1854 Broad Street investigation and his Southwark-Vauxhall versus Lambeth water-company comparison were explained away as irrelevant to the "foul air," and every epidemic could find a stench after the fact — the signature of under-determination, not derivation-first novelty.
- **P5 (F):** No predecessor was embedded with failure conditions: Fracastoro's contagion doctrine (*De Contagione*, 1546) had simply been assimilated, and the theory as a whole fell to refutation — Koch's comma bacillus (1883-84) and the Hamburg/Altona water contrast (1892) — not to demotion.
- **P6 (F):** Its one piece of formalism, Farr's elevation law (1852), was not reinterpreted but refuted — by the 1866 East London cholera traced to the East London Water Company's supplies — so formalism and meaning never came apart in its favor.
- **P7 (F):** No founder paid in his own coin: Chadwick's 1854 dismissal from the General Board of Health was political, and Nightingale, the doctrine's most eminent late holder (*Notes on Nursing*, 1859), defended foul-drain explanations against bacteriology into the 1880s at no conceptual cost to the theory itself.
- **P8 (S then D):** The miasma-driven sanitary program generated a real practical harvest — the 1848 Public Health Act, Bazalgette's London main drainage (1859-75), and the urban mortality declines of the sanitary revolution — but the theory degenerated from 1854 onward, unable to absorb Snow, Koch (1883-84), or the 1892 Hamburg epidemic, until its successor kept only the pipes.
- **P9 (F):** It opened no deeper floor: the doctrine dissolved under the bacteriological evidence rather than re-founding its subject, bequeathing infrastructure (sewers, water) but no sharpened conceptual problem — only the fossil word "malaria."

## T8 · Newtonian mechanics

- **P1 (S):** The *Principia* (1687) deleted the two-worlds cosmos: Book III extends the same laws to the Moon (the "moon test" of Prop. 4), to the comets (Props. 40-42), and to the tides, ending the Aristotelian division that had made the heavens and the Earth different sciences.
- **P2 (S):** The apple and the Moon were unified — the same inverse-square acceleration measured at the Earth's surface maintains the lunar orbit (*Principia*, Book III, Prop. 4, with its corollaries) — and the ocean tides joined the same dynamics as the planets.
- **P3 (S):** Universal gravitation installed the constant coupling of matter to matter — Book III, Prop. 7, gravity "proportional to the several quantities of matter which they contain," in all bodies universally — measured over a century later by Cavendish's torsion-balance determination of the Earth's density (1798).
- **P4 (S):** Newton needed almost no new observations — Kepler's laws (1609, 1619) and Galileo's kinematics sufficed — and the promissory notes were honored: Halley's 1705 *Synopsis* predicted the 1682 comet's return, seen by Palitzsch in December 1758 (perihelion March 1759), and Neptune was found at Berlin by Galle on Le Verrier's computed position (September 23, 1846).
- **P5 (S):** Kepler's area and ellipse laws became theorems derived from an inverse-square centripetal force (Book I), and the theory explained where the old astronomy must fail — in the mutual perturbations noted in the scholium to Book III, Prop. 13 (Jupiter upon Saturn), and in the Moon's inequalities — a limiting case with located failure conditions.
- **P6 (P):** Formalism and meaning came apart, but as abdication rather than reinterpretation: the General Scholium (1713) — "hypotheses non fingo" — let the inverse-square mathematics govern while renouncing any account of what attraction is, so the tradition ran two centuries on a formalism whose meaning was declared off-limits rather than made new.
- **P7 (S):** Newton surrendered the mechanical philosophy's ban on action at a distance and paid for it — Leibniz's letters to Clarke (1715-16) branded gravity an occult quality and a "perpetual miracle," and Huygens wrote to Leibniz (November 1690) that he was not satisfied by any theory built on Newton's principle of attraction.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest saturated: Maupertuis's Lapland expedition (1736-37) verified the predicted oblateness of the Earth against the Cassinis, Laplace's *Mécanique céleste* (1799-1825) built celestial mechanics into a science, and the promissory notes kept being honored — the comet of 1758-59, Neptune in 1846 — with the program still generating.
- **P9 (S):** Newton left the mechanism of gravity unexplained from 1687 to 1915 — he confessed in his third letter to Bentley (February 1693) that action at a distance through a vacuum is "so great an absurdity" that no competent thinker could believe it — a wound open for 228 years until general relativity closed it.

## T9 · Phlogiston chemistry

- **P1 (F):** The founding was additive: Stahl (from 1703) generalized Becher's *terra pinguis* (1669) into a new principle of inflammability, and the theory subtracted nothing — no prior conceptual necessity was made unthinkable by it.
- **P2 (P):** The theory genuinely unified combustion, calcination, smelting, and respiration under one transferable, conserved principle — metals as calx plus phlogiston, charcoal phlogiston-rich — the first chemistry-wide conserved substance; but the deep unification of those very phenomena (combustion as combination with oxygen) was achieved by its rival, Lavoisier (1783-89).
- **P3 (F):** No constant: phlogiston was never isolated, weighed, or measured — its quantity was a bookkeeping deficit — and nothing like G, c, or h entered chemistry with it.
- **P4 (F):** The theory was fitted and then contradicted by the data: the weight *gain* of calcined metals, remarked from Jean Rey's 1630 essay onward, was accommodated by granting phlogiston levity rather than predicted, and no promissory note was ever honored in its own terms.
- **P5 (F):** No predecessor was embedded: Stahl refined Becher without deriving any failure conditions, and the theory fell to outright refutation — Lavoisier's *Réflexions sur le phlogistique* (1783) called it a veritable Proteus changing shape to fit every case — not to demotion as a limiting case.
- **P6 (F):** The dissociation again ran against the theory: the same gravimetric and gas observations were held by phlogistians — Cavendish's inflammable air (1766), Priestley's dephlogisticated air (1774), even Cavendish's synthesis of water (1784), still reported phlogistically — while Lavoisier made them mean hydrogen and oxygen: the theory supplied the discarded meaning.
- **P7 (P):** The founders made no recorded surrender, but its late holders paid: Priestley defended phlogiston to his death (*The Doctrine of Phlogiston Established*, 1800), and Kirwan's *Essay on Phlogiston* (1787) was translated into French by Marie-Anne Lavoisier (1788) with point-by-point refutations printed beside it — resistance to the successor at real reputational cost.
- **P8 (S then D):** The generative phase was real — phlogiston reasoning organized the pneumatic chemistry that discovered the gases (Black's fixed air, 1754-57; Cavendish's inflammable air, 1766; Scheele's fire air, 1771-73; Priestley's dephlogisticated air, 1774) — then the program degenerated into defense as Lavoisier's balance-sheets won the 1780s and the theory died with its defenders' books.
- **P9 (P):** It organized eighteenth-century chemistry around a problem it could not close — the nature of its own principle — and its gas apparatus and discoveries became the floor on which the oxygen theory and stoichiometry were built; but the open question was fatal to the theory itself rather than a deeper floor it consciously handed on.

## T10 · Quantum mechanics

- **P1 (S):** The quantum deleted classical continuity and then determinism: Planck's radiation law (December 14, 1900, Verhandlungen der Deutschen Physikalischen Gesellschaft) introduced discontinuous energy exchange, and after Born's probabilistic reading of psi (1926) and Heisenberg's uncertainty relations (1927) the classical trajectory became physically unstatable.
- **P2 (S):** Wave and particle were unified — Einstein's 1905 photoelectric paper extended Planck's quantum to radiation itself, de Broglie extended it to matter (1923-24), and Davisson-Germer and G. P. Thomson's electron diffraction (1927) confirmed him — while Pauli's exclusion principle (1925) unified atomic structure with chemistry's periodic law.
- **P3 (S):** Planck's h entered as the universal quantum of action on December 14, 1900, and immediately re-scaled the conceptual system: Bohr's angular-momentum quantization (1913), the de Broglie relations (1924), and the uncertainty product (1927).
- **P4 (S):** The theory was over-determined by old data and under-determined by old theory — the Rubens-Kurlbaum infrared measurements (1900), Balmer's 1885 formula, Lenard's 1902 photoelectric results — and its promissory notes were honored: Bohr's 1913 atom derived the Rydberg constant from the then-known e, m, and h and predicted the 4686-angstrom line of ionized helium (confirmed by Fowler, 1913), Franck and Hertz found the discrete 4.9-volt mercury level (1914), Stern and Gerlach found space quantization (1922), and electron diffraction arrived in 1927.
- **P5 (S):** Classical physics was demoted to a limit with located failures: Bohr's correspondence principle (1913-22) recovered classical frequencies at large quantum numbers, and Ehrenfest's theorem (1927) carried Newtonian equations of motion into the limit h to 0.
- **P6 (S):** The profundity was interpretation of held formalism: Schrödinger published his equation in 1926 with psi a continuous matter-wave, and Born's June 1926 collision papers made the same squared modulus mean probability density — the mathematics unchanged, the world-claim transformed — just as Heisenberg's founding paper of 1925 was explicitly an *Umdeutung*, a reinterpretation, of kinematic relations.
- **P7 (S):** The founders paid in their own coin: Planck spent years trying to undo his surrender of continuity (his "second theory" of 1912 confined the discontinuity to emission), and Einstein declined the next surrender — the dice letter to Born (December 4, 1926) — spending thirty years from the Solvay debates (1927) through EPR (1935) resisting the probabilistic reading of the theory his photon had begun.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest saturated: the transistor (Bardeen, Brattain, and Shockley, 1947), the laser (Einstein's 1917 stimulated emission to Maiman's 1960 ruby device), solid-state electronics generally, and quantum information (Bell's inequalities, 1964; Aspect's Orsay experiments, 1982; the 2022 Nobel Prize to Clauser, Aspect, and Zeilinger).
- **P9 (S):** The measurement problem has been open since 1926-27 (Born's probabilistic reading and the October 1927 Solvay debates), through EPR (1935) and Bell (1964) to the present — a century, and counting.

## T11 · Relativity

- **P1 (S):** The 1905 paper *Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper* (June 30, 1905) declared the luminiferous ether "superfluous" and replaced absolute simultaneity with light-signal clock synchronization — two deletions that have held in physics ever since.
- **P2 (S):** The program dissolved three ancient joints: space and time (Minkowski's "Raum und Zeit," September 21, 1908), mass and energy (*Ist die Trägheit eines Körpers...*, November 1905), and gravity and geometry (the field equations presented to the Prussian Academy, November 25, 1915).
- **P3 (S):** c entered as the invariant limiting speed of all influence — the 1905 kinematics declared velocities greater than that of light "impossible" in principle — re-scaling mechanics (relativistic mass), electromagnetism (covariance), and eventually cosmology (light-cone structure).
- **P4 (S):** Special relativity needed the eighteen-year-old null result of Michelson and Morley (1887) plus old optics; general relativity retrodicted the known perihelion anomaly of Mercury (43 arcseconds per century, standing since Le Verrier's 1859 analysis; Einstein's communication of November 18, 1915) and issued promissory notes honored in 1919 (the Dyson-Eddington eclipse deflection, announced November 6, 1919) and in 2015 (LIGO's GW150914 detection, from the wave predictions of 1916-18).
- **P5 (S):** The predecessors became limits with failure conditions: Galilean kinematics as v much less than c in the velocity-addition theorem, and Newtonian gravity as the weak-field limit, each explaining why the old theory worked and where it must fail (Mercury's excess precession among the failures).
- **P6 (S):** Lorentz held the equations before Einstein — the transformations of 1895-1904 and the theorem of corresponding states, held by Lorentz as measurement-distortions relative to a preferred frame — and Einstein's profundity was making local time mean time and contraction mean kinematics: the same mathematics saying a new thing about the world.
- **P7 (S):** Einstein surrendered the ether of his youth (his 1899 letters to Mileva Maric still discuss experiments on motion relative to the ether; his 1920 Leiden lecture "Ether and Relativity" attests the reversal) — and then declined the quantum's demand: the dice letter to Born (1926) and EPR (1935), thirty years of resistance to his own revolution's successor.
- **P8 (S):** General relativity's harvest: modern cosmology (Friedmann 1922, Lemaitre 1927, Hubble 1929, the Penzias-Wilson background of 1965), black holes (Schwarzschild's 1916 solution to Cygnus X-1's 1971 identification to the Event Horizon Telescope's 2019 image), and gravitational-wave astronomy (LIGO, 2015).
- **P9 (S):** It closed Newton's 228-year wound — the mechanism of gravity — and opened the standing war with quantum mechanics over the black hole interior: singularities (Penrose's 1965 theorem), Hawking radiation (1974), and the information paradox (from 1975-76 to the present).

## T12 · Thermodynamics

- **P1 (S):** The theory deleted caloric: Joule's paddle-wheel experiments (1843-49) and Mayer's 1842 paper made the conserved heat-fluid untenable, and the second law (Clausius 1850, Thomson 1851) deleted the time-reversibility of macroscopic physics, installing irreversibility as a law of nature.
- **P2 (S):** Heat, work, electricity, and chemistry were unified as interconvertible energy — Joule's chain from the electrical heating law (1840) through the magneto-electric experiments (1843) to the paddle wheel (1845-49), generalized by Helmholtz's *Über die Erhaltung der Kraft* (1847) — revealing heat's separation from mechanics as an artifact of description.
- **P3 (P):** The tradition fixed conversion factors and numbers — Joule's mechanical equivalent (about 772 ft-lb per degree Fahrenheit, 1849), Rowland's redetermination (1879), Loschmidt's molecular count (1865) — but the constant of G/c/h rank, Boltzmann's k, received its value and role only in Planck's December 14, 1900 paper, exactly at this theory's boundary with quantum theory (Clausius's entropy of 1865 fixed only the ratio dS = dQ/T).
- **P4 (P):** The second law was derived from Carnot's old 1824 analysis with no new data, but the first law stood on Joule's new 1840s measurements; and the theory's deepest promissory notes were honored just past its boundary — the statistical reading vindicated by Brownian motion (Einstein 1905, Perrin 1908) and the Nernst heat theorem (1905-06).
- **P5 (S):** The theory embedded rather than refuted its predecessor's working core: Carnot's theorem survived the death of caloric intact (Clausius 1850; Thomson's 1848 absolute scale was built on it), and the consolidation made the old results limiting cases — the ideal gas law as the low-density limit of van der Waals's 1873 equation (failing at Andrews's 1869 critical phenomena), and macroscopic entropy as the large-N equilibrium limit of Boltzmann's statistics (1877), with failure conditions located in fluctuations (Maxwell's demon, 1867-71).
- **P6 (S):** Clausius's entropy (1865) kept its formal definition dS = dQ/T while Boltzmann's 1877 reading made the same quantity mean the logarithm of the probability of the molecular state — the profundity, irreversibility as probability, lived entirely in the interpretation of an unchanged formula.
- **P7 (S):** The founders paid in their own coin: Kelvin, formed in Carnot's and Regnault's caloric program (his 1848 absolute-scale paper is written wholly in caloric terms), broke with it in his 1851 "Dynamical Theory of Heat"; Helmholtz's 1847 memoir was refused by Poggendorff's *Annalen* and had to be self-published; Mayer collapsed after the priority neglect (suicide attempt, 1850); Boltzmann died by suicide in 1906 still fighting for the atomic reading that Kelvin resisted to the end (the "Nineteenth-Century Clouds" lecture, 1900).
- **P8 (S):** The harvest saturated: Gibbs's heterogeneous equilibrium and phase rule (1876-78), van't Hoff's osmotic pressure (1886) and Arrhenius's dissociation (1887) founding physical chemistry, Diesel's Carnot-limit engine (1897), Kamerlingh Onnes's helium liquefaction (1908), and eventually black-hole thermodynamics (Bekenstein 1972, Hawking 1974) — still generating.
- **P9 (S):** It closed the Carnot-Joule conflict of the 1840s (Clausius 1850, Thomson 1851) and immediately opened deeper crises — the molecular meaning of entropy (Loschmidt's reversibility paradox, 1876; Zermelo's recurrence objection, 1896), the arrow of time in a reversible mechanics, and the specific-heat and blackbody anomalies that became the quantum theory in December 1900.

## PART 3 — POINT NOTES

- **P6 (formalism and meaning) — the agent-ambiguity, exposed by T6.** The point's own exemplar straddles two theories in this panel: "Lorentz held the equations" belongs to T6, "before Einstein" belongs to T11. Coded as written ("the profundity *was* an interpretation"), the scaffold fails (T6 F) and the reinterpreter satisfies (T11 S) on the strength of the *same historical facts*. The same structure forced F for T1 (Carnot's 1824 mathematics preserved by Clapeyron in 1834) and T9 (Cavendish's 1784 water synthesis, phlogistically framed). The point also presumes mathematical formalism: Darwin's record shows the same structure over descriptive apparatus (Owen's homologies made to mean ancestry), which I coded P rather than S only because the point says "mathematics." Finally, Newton exposes a third variant: the 1713 General Scholium withholds meaning ("hypotheses non fingo") rather than supplying a new one — formalism and meaning come apart with the meaning left *empty*. The point conflates dissociation (they come apart) with reinterpretation (a new meaning is installed); only the latter is the profundity test.

- **P1 (deletion) — permanence and scope, exposed by T6 and T1.** The test is "what it makes unthinkable *afterward*," but deletions can be undone: the wave-ether program genuinely deleted Newton's emission theory (1819, the Poisson-Arago spot), yet the photon reinstated corpuscular light in 1905 — is an 86-year deletion still a deletion? And scope lines do real work: Lavoisier's 1789 *Traité* (cited in T1's scope) *did* perform a deletion — of phlogiston — but that act belongs to the oxygen-combustion theory, not to the caloric doctrine as scoped, so T1 coded F on a deletion its founding text physically contains. "The theory" is not always a well-carved unit of historical action.

- **P2 (unification) — the bar problem, exposed by T9 and T7.** Every framework unifies *something*: phlogiston genuinely unified combustion, calcination, and metallurgy under one conserved principle (the first in chemistry), and miasma unified the fevers into Farr's "zymotic" class (1852). I discriminated by distinguishing structural joint-dissolutions (heat/work, light/electromagnetism, man/beast) from substance-postulates and single-cause generalizations — but the definition does not force that distinction; a coder more sympathetic to substance-unifications would score T1, T9, and T7 higher, and Hasok Chang's rehabilitation of phlogiston shows the choice is interpretive, not mechanical.

- **P3 (universal constant) — loose definition versus closed exemplar list, exposed by T12.** "A constant that re-scales the conceptual system" is broader than the exemplar list (G, c, h): Joule's mechanical equivalent (1849), Loschmidt's number (1865), and Boltzmann's k all have claims — but k was named and fixed only in Planck's December 14, 1900 paper, which falls precisely on T12/T10's shared boundary, and h is claimed there by T10. Note also that c itself was *measured* by the ether program's era (Fizeau 1849) and made *fundamental* only in 1905 — "installation" turns out to be an act of interpretation, quietly reusing P6.

- **P4 (derivation-first) — "old data" versus new technique, exposed by T5 and T12.** Is a new laboratory technique a "new observation" in the Newton/Einstein sense? Germ theory was over-determined by old clinical observations (Bassi 1835, Snow 1854, Semmelweis 1847) but was won only with Koch's solid-culture and staining methods (1881) — hence P. Thermodynamics is split internally: the second law is derivation-first (from Carnot's 1824 thought experiment, no new data), the first law experiment-first (Joule 1843-49) — a single theory with two epistemic signatures, which the point does not anticipate.

- **P5 versus P1 — a built-in tension, exposed by T5 and T12.** P1 rewards deleting the predecessor; P5 requires *not* refuting it outright. Theories facing ontologically wrong but practically successful predecessors (miasma for germ theory; caloric for thermodynamics) split the difference: I coded T12 S because its embedding was formal (Carnot's theorem preserved exactly; the ideal gas law derived as van der Waals's limit) and T5 P because its embedding was practical (sanitary engineering vindicated; miasma refuted). But "predecessor" does double duty between ontology and phenomenology, and the instrument's exemplars all enjoyed phenomenologically victorious predecessors, which miasma and caloric were not.

- **P7 (founders' coin) — who is a founder, and what is a coin, exposed by T3 and T12.** For a millennium-long tradition (T3) there is no founder-figure who could pay anything — an F on absence of evidence. For multi-founder theories the "coin" came in two currencies that the definition does not separate: surrendering a held commitment (Kelvin's 1848-51 break with the caloric he inherited from Carnot and Regnault; Einstein's ether) versus *suffering* for the theory (Mayer's 1850 collapse and asylum; Boltzmann's 1906 suicide; Semmelweis's 1865 death, for a precursor). The definition demands the former; the record keeps offering the latter.

- **P8 (S then D) — ontology versus program, exposed by T6.** After 1887 the ether *ontology* degenerated (contraction patches, the 1904 patchwork) while the Maxwell-Lorentz *formal core* in the same scope kept generating (Zeeman 1896, Drude 1900) and then merged into the successor rather than running dry. "Degeneration-by-absorption" is not what Lakatos's term, or this note, describes. Phase windows are also scope-determined: Darwin's S survives partly because the "eclipse" (c. 1890-1920) is a later phase and the synthesis explicitly excluded — a different scope line could justify "S then D" there too.

- **P9 (crisis-chaining) — floors handed versus floors salvaged, exposed by T6, T1, T9.** Several defeated theories left successors working floors: the Lorentz group (salvaged by Einstein), Carnot's theorem (salvaged by Clausius), the gas discoveries (salvaged by Lavoisier's school). I coded these P and reserved S for theories whose own program re-founded the subject; but the definition's phrase "hands its successors a deeper floor to build on" does not distinguish a bequest consciously made from a corpse usefully looted, and reasonable coders could move T6, T1, and T9 up or T2 down on exactly that line.

**Summary for the orchestrator:** the instrument separates the panel cleanly into three tiers on its own criteria — the canonical deep theories (T10, T11: 9/9; T8: 8/9; T12: 7/9 with two near-misses at scope boundaries) — a middle band (T2: 6 S; T5: 4 S) — and the failed frameworks (T1, T3, T4, T7, T9: no S outside P8, all "S then D").
