L1
CONTENT
New Phenomena
novelty of content
New facts arrive that the existing framework must accommodate. The framework itself is not touched.
e.g.  the Michelson–Morley null result (1887) · the blackbody spectrum · atomic spectral lines
deeper conceptual reconstruction
L2
STRUCTURE
New Laws
novelty of structure
New relations connect the facts — equations and mechanisms that organize them under one rule.
e.g.  Newton’s laws of motion (1687) · the Lorentz transformations (1904) · matrix & wave mechanics (1925–26)
deeper conceptual reconstruction
L3
VOCABULARY
New Meta‑Concepts
novelty of vocabulary
The basic terms in which laws are written are themselves rewritten — space, time, cause, state, observation, explanation.
e.g.  heaven and earth as one world · the relativity of simultaneity · the death of determinism
