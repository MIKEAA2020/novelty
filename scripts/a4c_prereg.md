## Locked coding — MIASMA THEORY (dead side)

P1 Deletion at birth: **F** — the frame inherited its constitutive concept
(disease from corrupted air) from the Hippocratic corpus through Galen and
Sydenham's epidemic constitutions; the nineteenth-century version deleted
nothing at its christening — it WAS the inherited frame, refined by patches
(contingent contagionism) rather than deletions. Core posit = effluvia,
inherited.

P2 Unification: **P** — vocabulary-level, not law-level: the filth-fevers
unified as the zymotic class (Farr's classification, GRO returns) under one
cause-type, decomposing organic matter in foul air; no law of nature
unified; the deepest division of the domain — a specific living cause vs. a
generic environmental condition — was left untouched (and was the division
that mattered).

P3 Universal constant: **F** — no quantitative core; no invariant ever
installed; no unit of miasma was ever named or measured; Farr's 1849
elevation-temperature cholera relation was fitted to the epidemic and
abandoned when it failed; the program's numbers (mortality rates) measured
the disease, never the cause.

P4 Derivation-first: **F** — content retrofits, not structure: each
epidemic's signature absorbed by new local properties of the effluvium
(river-valley exposure, soil and groundwater states — Pettenkofer's 1869
Boden variant was itself a retrofit); Snow's 1854 water-company data
available in the program's own archive was patched (miasma from the
polluted river) rather than re-derived; nothing was ever derived that
yielded an invariant.

P5 Conservative embedding: **P** (machinery survives; ontology deleted;
successor-recovery = F) — the machinery survives as public-health apparatus
(sewerage, the 1848/1875 Public Health Acts, vital registration — death of
ontology, not of apparatus, exactly CR-2); but germ theory recovers miasma
as no limit of anything: foul odor is a symptom of microbial decomposition,
not a cause of disease; the practical wisdom (cleanliness works) is
re-derived for new reasons, which is not a limiting case.

P6 Formalism/meaning (operationalized: minted measurables): **F** — no
formalism existed to detach from a meaning; the theory demanded no
quantity that any instrument was built to measure; no miasma-meter was ever
standardized, no exposure unit ever named; the vital-statistics apparatus
measured deaths (public health), not the theoretical entity.

P7 Founders' payment-not-defense: **F** — Farr defended and adapted the
atmospheric reading across decades, converting late without ever
surrendering a held commitment to his own theory's implications; Chadwick
held the sanitary miasmatism to his death (1890); Pettenkofer's 1892
self-experiment — drinking cholera vibrio to defend his groundwater patch
— is defense at the price of the body itself, and he surrendered nothing.
Defense is not payment.

P8 Generative slope: **S then D** — generative phase: the sanitary movement
(1842 Chadwick report, the GRO, the Public Health Acts, sewerage and
waterworks) — a genuinely generative public-works century whose harvest is
still alive; degenerating phase: contingent contagionism, Pettenkofer's
localist variants, each new epidemiological signature (cholera's
waterborne signature, the 1854 South London company data) paid for with
new local properties of the effluvium; by Koch (1883) the program was
paying with its defenders' conversions.

P9 Crisis-chaining: **P** — closed the period's fever question in
vocabulary (the poor die of fever because they breathe filth); opened the
etiological question that killed it — the demand for a specific causal
agent — which was productive for the successor (Snow, Koch), the same
pattern as the other dead theories.

Miasma profile: F P F F P F F SD P — zero clean satisfied cells; fails all
five discriminators (deletion, constant, successor-recovery embedding,
minted measurables, slope-trend). Weakest dead theory in the census.

## Locked coding — GERM THEORY (living side, new resident)

P1 Deletion: **S** — a double deletion: miasma's etiological primitive
(disease-causing effluvia) AND the spontaneous-generation allowance (the
1861 swan-neck flasks); "bad air causes plague" became unthinkable as a
cause-category after Koch; the core posit (a replication-qualified,
species-specific agent) was new, not inherited.

P2 Unification: **S** — law-level: one mechanism class — the
replication-qualified agent — across fermentation, putrefaction, wound
infection, epidemic disease (and later nitrogen fixation, genetics); the
distinction dissolved was natural, ancient, and obvious — the brewery vat
and the sickbed (industry and medicine) revealed as the same phenomenon;
the deepest unification in the history of biology.

P3 Universal constant: **F** as written — no dimensionful universal
constant was installed; no G/c/h analog exists in germ theory; the nearest
candidates are methodological invariants (Koch's postulates as causal
criteria, 1882) and taxonomic principles (one disease, one agent), neither
a constant of nature that re-scales the conceptual system; genetic-code
universality belongs to the successor harvest (molecular biology), not the
founding. PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTATION: this cell breaks the living side's
unanimity for the first time and forces a domain-scope adjudication (see
E3 below).

P4 Derivation-first: **S** — over-determined by old data: Snow's 1854
Broad Street and South London data, Semmelweis's 1847 cadaveric
contamination statistics, the Lille distillery infections Pasteur already
knew (1856-57) — all re-derived under the new frame without new
instruments; promissory notes honored on the century scale (the specific
agent for every epidemic disease: influenza 1933, poliomyelitis 1949, ...

P5 Conservative embedding: **P** — the practice is recovered (sanitation,
sewerage, quarantine re-derived as removing breeding media and interrupting
transmission), the ontology is deleted; there is no parameter limit in
which "foul air causes disease" becomes true — practical-wisdom recovery
without formal subsumption; the Newton-to-relativity mathematical limit
has no analog here.

P6 Formalism/meaning (operationalized): **S** — minted measurables at full
strength: the causal organism itself — stainable (Gram, 1884), countable
(colony-forming units), cultivable (Koch's solid media, 1881; the Petri
dish, 1887); instruments built because the theory demanded them: the
swan-neck flask (1861), the autoclave, the Chamberland filter (1884); and
one coherent discriminating list: the postulates — a list that could
return and did return negative results.

P7 Founders' payment-not-defense: **P** — real but partial payment:
Pasteur surrendered the chemical theory of fermentation he inherited from
his own chemical formation (Liebig's authority) when the Lille
micro-observations forced the vital process on him (1857-58); Koch paid
publicly and expensively when his own tuberculin "cure" (1890) failed his
own postulates' standard; but a Newton-or-Planck-grade surrender of a
constitutive commitment to his own theory's deepest implication is not
cleanly documented for either founder.

P8 Generative slope: **S** — virology (the filterable agents, 1892-98),
immunology (antitoxins 1890, serotherapy), the vaccine institutes (rabies
1885, the Pasteur Institute), antibiotics (penicillin 1928,
streptomycin 1943), molecular genetics (transformation 1928/1944, phage
work feeding the double helix), and still generative now (CRISPR is
bacterial immunity). A century-plus harvest nobody ordered.

P9 Crisis-chaining: **S** — closed a two-millennium crisis (what causes
plagues — and why the poor die of them); opened deeper floors: why does
the agent not always cause disease (the host side — immunology), what is
the agent's chemistry (molecular biology), what passes the filter
(virology).

Germ profile: S S F S P S P S S — six satisfied, two partial, one failed.
