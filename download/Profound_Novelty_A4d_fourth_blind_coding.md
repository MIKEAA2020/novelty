## PART 2 — EVIDENCE LINES

### T1 · The caloric theory of heat

- **P1 (F):** Lavoisier's *Traité élémentaire de chimie* (1789) simply **added** "calorique" at the head of the table of simple substances, in the established imponderable-fluid fashion (alongside lumière), and the kinetic alternative stayed thinkable throughout (Rumford's 1798 Munich cannon-boring; Davy's 1799 ice-rubbing).
- **P2 (P):** Laplace's 1816 speed-of-sound correction, on Poisson's adiabatic relation, made Black's latent heat (c. 1761), gas elasticity, and sound one fluid's business — but the unity was the postulated substance itself, later shown to be the artifact.
- **P3 (F):** No constant was installed: Carnot's 1824 μ(T) was an unknown function (which Kelvin's 1848 absolute scale could use but not evaluate), and Black's specific and latent heats are material properties, not universals.
- **P4 (P):** Laplace's 1816 correction re-derived a 130-year-old anomaly (Newton's *Principia* sound-speed shortfall) from caloric adiabatics, and Carnot's 1824 efficiency theorem was honored by Clausius (1850) — but the fluid-postulate itself was never forced by data and was defended against Joule's 1843–49 equivalents only by ad hoc "diminished capacities."
- **P5 (P):** The successor kept the phenomenology — Black's calorimetry, Regnault's specific heats, Clapeyron's 1834 equation, even Kelvin's 1848 absolute scale derived from Carnot's caloric axiom — while deleting the substance, entropy behaving like conserved caloric only in the reversible limit.
- **P6 (P):** Clapeyron's 1834 memoir had already detached Carnot's cycle from the caloric argument, and Carnot's posthumous notes (published 1878) abandoned conservation of heat while keeping the analysis — the mathematics was portable before its meaning changed, but the meaning-change belonged to the successor.
- **P7 (F):** Its makers installed it at no belief-cost — Lavoisier listed caloric as an element in 1789 like any other — and Carnot's private recantation (the 1878-published notes, with his ~370 kg·m per kilocalorie mechanical-equivalent estimate near Joule's value) was a founder's defection, not a costly surrender.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for sixty years — the calorimetry under Watt's engine era, Carnot (1824), Clapeyron (1834), Regnault's precision program (1840s) — then degenerating: Rumford (1798) and Davy (1799) had to be explained away, and Joule's 1843–49 equivalents were met with ad hoc capacity changes until Clausius and Thomson re-founded the subject without the fluid (1850–51).
- **P9 (P):** It closed the crisis of making heat measurable (Black's calorimetry, c. 1761), and Carnot's caloric analysis set the question his successors answered — but the deeper tension it harbored (conserved heat versus produced work) surfaced only in 1824 and was an anomaly it could not contain, not a floor it knowingly left open.

### T2 · The epicycle tradition (Ptolemaic astronomy)

- **P1 (F):** The *Almagest* (2nd century CE) accumulated — Apollonius's epicycles and eccenters, Hipparchus's data and solar theory — and Ptolemy's *Planetary Hypotheses* even re-attached physical spheres; nothing was made unthinkable.
- **P2 (P):** One geometrical method (deferent–epicycle–equant, *Almagest* IX–XIII) covered all seven wanderers and delivered eclipse prediction (Book VI) — a procedural unification, but each planet kept its own apparatus and no identity of natures was revealed.
- **P3 (F):** Its numbers — precession (1°/century), obliquity, apogees, epicycle ratios — were fitted parameters, never a new universal constant.
- **P4 (F):** The models were curve-fitted (to Ptolemy's and Hipparchus's observations), and the long-known equivalence of epicycle and eccentric representations (Apollonius's theorem) shows under-determination rather than derivation-first; there were no promissory notes beyond in-design eclipse accuracy.
- **P5 (P):** The successor explained why the machinery worked — a superior planet's epicycle encodes Earth's orbital motion — and where it fails (Venus's full phases, Galileo 1610), but Ptolemaic cosmology was largely refuted rather than demoted to a stated validity regime.
- **P6 (P):** The same deferent–epicycle geometry carried opposite cosmological meanings — Copernicus's lunar and Mercury models in *De Revolutionibus* (1543) are geometrically twinned with Ibn al-Shāṭir's Damascus models (c. 1350) — but within the tradition that detachability was deferral (mathematical astronomy vs. physical spheres), not profundity.
- **P7 (P):** Ptolemy paid a real coin when the equant broke uniform circular motion about the deferent's center — the compromise that al-Biṭrūjī (c. 1200) and Copernicus (*Commentariolus*, c. 1510) both treated as the tradition's wound — though this is a technical, not a worldview, surrender.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for 1,400 years — the Handy Tables, the Islamic *zīj* tradition, eclipse theory, the Alfonsine Tables (printed 1483) carrying Latin astronomy to the Gregorian reform (1582) — but by c. 1510 Copernicus's *Commentariolus* still needed 34 circles without accuracy gains, and Tycho's 1572 nova and 1577 comet broke the spheres the models presupposed.
- **P9 (P):** It re-founded mathematical astronomy but handed down a blemish — the equant's physical implausibility, which the Maragha school labored to remove (Ṭūsī's couple, 1259; al-ʿUrḍī; Ibn al-Shāṭir) — while the genuinely physical question (what moves the planets) was assigned out to Aristotelian cosmology rather than opened deeper.

### T3 · Germ theory of disease

- **P1 (S):** Pasteur's swan-neck flask experiments (1861, against Pouchet's 1859 claims) made spontaneous generation untenable, and after Koch's comma-bacillus work (1883–84) foul air ceased to be an admissible cause of epidemic cholera.
- **P2 (S):** One mechanism carried across the thought-to-be-separate — Lille's "lactic ferment" (1857), the silkworm pébrine (1865–70), anthrax and rabies in the 1870s–80s — dissolving the boundary between industrial chemistry and medicine.
- **P3 (F):** The program installed methods and criteria — Koch's postulates (1882–84), Petri's dish (1887), Gram's stain (1884) — but no universal constant; there is no germ-theoretic analog of G, c, or h.
- **P4 (P):** It retro-explained old results nobody had grounded (Jenner's 1796 vaccination; Snow's 1854 Southwark–Lambeth comparison; Semmelweis's 1847 cadaveric-handwashing result), but its founding ran on new technique and new data — Koch's 1876 mouse-to-mouse anthrax life cycle by pure culture — not on derivation from the old record alone.
- **P5 (P):** The successor embedded the practice, not the doctrine: germ theory explained why sanitation worked (the 1892 Hamburg–Altona contrast — same Elbe water, one city filtered, roughly 8,600 cholera deaths in Hamburg against a handful in Altona), but "putrescent miasma" itself received no domain of validity.
- **P6 (F):** The theory is not formalism-bearing — its content is etiological specificity demonstrated by technique (Koch's 1882 isolation of the tubercle bacillus on solid media) — and no "same equations, new meaning" episode exists in its record.
- **P7 (F):** Its makers paid no founding coin; the coin ran the other way — the 1855 Board of Health report rejected Snow's pump-handle evidence, and Semmelweis was driven from Vienna to his death in 1865 — the costs were paid by the theory's forerunners against the incumbent doctrine.
- **P8 (S):** Still saturating 130 years on — Behring and Kitasato's antitoxins (1890), the filterable viruses (Ivanovsky 1892; Beijerinck 1898; Löffler–Frosch 1898), penicillin (Fleming 1928; Florey's clinical trials 1940–43), and Avery–MacLeod–McCarty (1944), which turned the germ lineage into molecular genetics.
- **P9 (S):** It closed the contagion–miasma war and immediately opened deeper floors — why the exposed host resists (Metchnikoff's phagocytes, 1883–84; the 1890 antitoxins), agents below Koch's resolution (the viruses), and the host–agent equilibrium — the immunological and molecular re-founding still running.

### T4 · The luminiferous ether

- **P1 (F):** The ether was the era's great addition — a medium postulated to make waves possible (Huygens, *Traité de la Lumière*, written 1678, published 1690) — and the thing it eventually made unthinkable was itself (Einstein's "superfluous," 1905).
- **P2 (P):** The program achieved a real unification — light as electromagnetic disturbance (Maxwell 1865, whose displacement current was motivated by his 1861–62 vortex-ether model) — but the unifying medium proved to be the artifact: the light=EM identification survives the ether's deletion, so the unification is real while the theory's own unifier is not.
- **P3 (F):** Fizeau (1849) and Foucault (1850) measured light's speed as a property of propagation — frame-relative by construction, the whole point of the 1881/87 drift experiments — so c's installation as a universal constant belongs to 1905, not to the ether.
- **P4 (P):** Genuine derivation-first episodes — Fresnel's 1818 dragging coefficient, conjectured from Arago's 1810 refraction null and confirmed by Fizeau's 1851 water experiments; Maxwell's c-ratio from the 1856 Weber–Kohlrausch measurement, honored by Hertz (1887) — but the load-bearing postulate (the medium's rest frame) issued no derivation and failed its decisive test (Michelson–Morley, 1887).
- **P5 (P):** Special relativity kept the entire Lorentz–Maxwell formalism — the 1895/1904 transformations became kinematics — but granted the medium no domain: Lorentz's first-order ether electrodynamics is exactly SR to O(v/c), yet the ether qua medium received only superfluousness, not a regime of validity.
- **P6 (P):** The mature formalism was held under a dying meaning — Voigt (1887), Lorentz (1895, 1904), and Poincaré (July 1905) held the transformations with the ether still in the physics — but the meaning-change that mattered fell in 1905, outside the theory's own scope, so within T4 the separation is visible without the profundity being its own.
- **P7 (P):** Maxwell paid a founder's coin when the 1865 "Dynamical Theory" dropped his own 1861–62 vortex-and-idle-wheel mechanism for medium-agnostic Lagrangian dynamics — but Huygens and Fresnel paid nothing at installation (Fresnel risked career, not creed, in the 1818 Paris prize competition), and the endgame costs (Lorentz still lecturing an ether reading in *The Theory of Electrons*, 1909) were paid for resistance, not for the theory.
- **P8 (S then D):** Enormously generative — wave optics after Young (1801–04) and Fresnel (1815–27), spectroscopy, Maxwell–Hertz electromagnetism and radio (Marconi, 1901) — then degenerating under the null cascade (Michelson 1881; Michelson–Morley 1887; Rayleigh–Brace 1902–04; Trouton–Noble 1903), each null bought off with a new ad hoc property (FitzGerald 1889; Lorentz 1892) until 1905 cut the medium away.
- **P9 (S):** It closed the century-old corpuscle–wave contest (the 1818 Poisson spot; Foucault's 1850 water-speed measurement) and its own success opened the deeper question — the medium's state of motion — whose pursuit (the drift experiments of 1881–1905) created the very crisis that special relativity closed and deepened.

### T5 · Miasma theory of disease

- **P1 (F):** The doctrine was inherited, not founded — the Hippocratic *Airs, Waters, Places* tradition running through Chadwick's 1842 *Report on the Sanitary Condition of the Labouring Population* — and it deleted nothing.
- **P2 (F):** Its unity (all fevers from one environmental cause) was the ancient Hippocratic frame itself, and Farr's 1852 cholera–elevation law was a fitted correlation later re-read as water-supply contamination; no distinction was revealed as an artifact.
- **P3 (F):** No constant: the program's quantitative legacy (Farr's Registrar-General returns; Pettenkofer's soil-and-groundwater measurements, 1869–73) never condensed into a universal number.
- **P4 (F):** It was curve-fitting to epidemic patterns (Farr's 1852 elevation regression) with mis-directed inference — the 1855 Board of Health report explicitly rejected Snow's waterborne evidence — and it issued no promissory note that was ever honored.
- **P5 (P):** The successor embedded the practice, not the doctrine: germ theory explained why the sanitary program worked (reservoir and water contamination — Nightingale's miasma-driven army sanitation had demonstrably cut mortality in the 1855 Sanitary Commission data), but "foul air from putrescence" itself got no validity domain.
- **P6 (F):** There was no formalism to part from meaning — the theory's content was qualitative etiology plus sanitary engineering.
- **P7 (F):** No maker paid a founding coin: the movement's costs were political (Chadwick's General Board of Health was reorganized away over him, 1854), and the coin paid at the end ran the wrong way — Pettenkofer's 1892 self-ingestion of Koch's cholera vibrio, defending the soil theory to his own scientific ruin.
- **P8 (S then D):** The sanitary phase harvested real, unordered returns — Bazalgette's London main drainage (1859–65) after the Great Stink of 1858, Nightingale's army reforms (her 1858 *Notes* and the Army Sanitary Commission) — measurable in epidemic mortality, then the theory degenerated against bacteriology: after Koch's 1884 cholera work, Pettenkofer's 1892 stunt cost him his standing and returned nothing.
- **P9 (F):** It closed no crisis and opened no deeper floor — it faded; even its last theoretical gesture, Pettenkofer's groundwater theory (1869–73), generated no successor program.

### T6 · Newtonian mechanics

- **P1 (S):** The *Principia* (1687) deleted the two-worlds cosmos — the Moon-test of Book III made celestial and terrestrial one dynamical system — and Book II demolished the Cartesian vortices, shown inconsistent with Kepler's third law.
- **P2 (S):** The apple and the Moon: the same inverse-square acceleration that bends a projectile holds the Moon in its orbit (the Book III Moon-test), revealing the sublunary/superlunary divide as an artifact of description.
- **P3 (S):** Universal gravitation installed G — the coupling of any two masses (Book I's proportionality; Cavendish's 1798 torsion-balance measurement of the mean density of the Earth).
- **P4 (S):** Built from old results (Kepler's laws, Galileo's kinematics) with almost no new observation, its promissory notes were honored for 160 years — Halley's comet returned on schedule (sighted by Palitzsch, December 1758), Maupertuis's Lapland geodesy (1737) confirmed the predicted oblateness, and Neptune was found on paper by Le Verrier and caught by Galle (23 September 1846).
- **P5 (S):** It subsumed rather than refuted Kepler — the ellipse is the exact two-body solution, with perturbation theory saying where Kepler must fail (the lunar inequalities of Book III; the great Jupiter–Saturn inequality that Laplace resolved, 1785–86).
- **P6 (P):** The inverse-square law itself was common currency before 1687 — Halley, Wren, and Hooke all had it from Kepler III plus Huygens's 1673 centrifugal measure in the *Horologium Oscillatorium*, and Halley's 1684 Cambridge visit posed the orbit question — but the profundity was Newton's own new demonstrations and systematization, not a re-meaning of held mathematics.
- **P7 (S):** Newton surrendered the mechanical philosophy's ban on action at a distance, refusing to feign a mechanism ("hypotheses non fingo," General Scholium, 1713), and was attacked as an occultist for it by Leibniz and Huygens (Huygens's letters to Leibniz, 1690).
- **P8 (S):** Celestial mechanics harvested for a century and never degenerated — the lunar theory under the *Nautical Almanac* (1767), Laplace's *Mécanique Céleste*, Neptune (1846) — and the machinery still flies spacecraft today.
- **P9 (S):** It closed the crisis of planetary motion and knowingly left its floor open — the mechanism of gravity itself — a wound that stayed open 228 years until general relativity closed it (1915).

### T7 · Phlogiston chemistry

- **P1 (F):** Stahl's 1703 installation renamed and extended Becher's *terra pinguis* (*Physica Subterranea*, 1669) — an addition to chemistry's principle-vocabulary that deleted nothing and made nothing unthinkable.
- **P2 (P):** It genuinely unified combustion, calcination, and respiration under one transferable principle (metals as calx-plus-phlogiston, making metallurgy the same kind of process as fire) — but the unity was the postulated substance itself, and the successor inverted rather than deepened it.
- **P3 (F):** No constant — its quantitative practice never fixed a universal number; the balance, which Lavoisier would weaponize in the 1780s, was phlogiston's enemy, not its instrument.
- **P4 (F):** It was accommodative postulation: the pneumatic discoveries were digested by renaming (Cavendish's 1766 "inflammable air" was identified with phlogiston itself; Priestley's 1774 "dephlogisticated air" inverted oxygen), and the weight-gain of calcined metals (established by Guyton de Morveau, 1772) forced the ad hoc of buoyant or massless phlogiston — no honored promissory notes.
- **P5 (F):** The successor refuted it outright: Lavoisier's 1783 attack on the theory, the 1787 *Méthode de nomenclature chimique* (which omitted phlogiston from the new vocabulary), and the 1789 *Traité* gave it no domain, no limit, no survival.
- **P6 (F):** It had no formalism to be re-meant — its content was qualitative principle-talk; the balance-sheet formalism was the rival's innovation (Lavoisier's conservation of mass, 1789).
- **P7 (F):** Its makers paid no founding coin (Stahl prospered at Halle and at the Prussian court in Berlin), and its endgame loyalty — Priestley's 1796 *Considerations on the Doctrine of Phlogiston*, published from Pennsylvania, where he died in 1804 still phlogistonian — is the refusal to pay, the pattern in reverse.
- **P8 (S then D):** Generative for decades — the entire pneumatic revolution was harvested under its guidance (Black's fixed air, 1754–56; Cavendish 1766; Priestley's gases 1772–74; Scheele's fire-air, made c. 1771–73 and published 1777) — then degenerating: the weight anomaly and the post-1785 defenses (negative-weight phlogiston) paid more for each result than it returned until the 1789 *Traité* closed the program.
- **P9 (F):** It left no deeper floor: its successor built on the balance and the new nomenclature, not on any question phlogiston opened — the one deep question it gestured at (what is common to fire and life?) was answered by the rival (respiration as slow combustion, Lavoisier and Laplace's ice-calorimetry, 1780–83).

### T8 · Quantum mechanics

- **P1 (S):** It deleted determinism — Born's June 1926 probability reading made individual events irreducibly chancy, and the 1927 Solvay confrontation with Einstein marks exactly where the old thinkability died.
- **P2 (S):** Wave and particle — de Broglie's 1924 matter waves, confirmed by Davisson–Germer (1927), made the particle/wave distinction an artifact of description rather than a joint in nature.
- **P3 (S):** h — Planck's December 1900 derivation fixed the quantum of action, re-scaling the whole conceptual system from spectroscopy to uncertainty (ΔxΔp ≥ ħ/2, 1927).
- **P4 (S):** Over-determined by old data (Balmer's 1885 formula retro-derived by Bohr in 1913; the 1897–1900 Berlin blackbody measurements forcing Planck's formula before its derivation) and honored across a century — Einstein's 1917 coefficients to Maiman's laser (1960), Bose–Einstein (1924) to the condensate (1995), Dirac (1928) to Anderson's positron (1932).
- **P5 (S):** The correspondence principle (Bohr, 1913–23) and Ehrenfest's theorem (1927) made classical behavior the large-quantum-number limit — the predecessor explained as a regime, not refuted.
- **P6 (S):** Schrödinger held the 1926 equation as classical matter-wave theory while Born's collision papers attached |ψ|² — the same mathematics made to say a new thing (Schrödinger protested the "damned quantum jumping" to Bohr in Copenhagen, 1926).
- **P7 (S):** Its founders paid in their own coin — Planck's two-decade fight against the discontinuity he himself had introduced (the "act of desperation" of his *Scientific Autobiography*) and Einstein's thirty-year resistance to quantum indeterminism (Solvay 1927 and 1930; the EPR paper, 1935).
- **P8 (S):** Still saturating — the transistor (Bardeen–Brattain–Shockley, 1947), the laser, Bell's 1964 theorem and Aspect's 1982 tests, quantum information — a technological century nobody ordered in 1900 or 1926.
- **P9 (S):** It left the measurement problem open for a century and counting (from Solvay 1927 through the decoherence program), and its war with general relativity over the black hole interior (Hawking, 1974–76) is the deepest floor handed to successors.

### T9 · Relativity

- **P1 (S):** Special relativity deleted the ether and absolute simultaneity in 1905 (the relativity of simultaneity derived from the two postulates), and general relativity deleted gravitational force in favor of geometry (1915).
- **P2 (S):** Space and time into spacetime (Minkowski, "Raum und Zeit," 1908), mass and energy (E = mc², September 1905), gravity and geometry (the November 1915 field equations) — three ancient distinctions revealed as artifacts.
- **P3 (S):** c installed as the cap on the propagation of influence (the 1905 second postulate) — the constant that re-scales mechanics and, from 1908, spacetime itself.
- **P4 (S):** An eighteen-year-old null result (Michelson–Morley, 1887) and a fifty-six-year-old anomaly (Le Verrier's 1859 Mercury perihelion, 43″/century) sufficed, and the promissory notes were honored at 4, 44, and 100 years — light bending (Dyson–Eddington, 1919), the Pound–Rebka redshift (1959), and gravitational waves (LIGO, 2015).
- **P5 (S):** Newtonian kinematics survives as the v/c → 0 limit and Newtonian gravity as the weak-field limit of the 1915 equations — the predecessor explained and bounded, not refuted.
- **P6 (S):** Lorentz held the equations before Einstein made them kinematics (1895, 1904; Voigt 1887; Poincaré July 1905), and Hilbert submitted his field-equation paper in Göttingen (20 November 1915) five days before Einstein's communication of 25 November — the profundity lived in the meaning, not in the formulas.
- **P7 (S):** Einstein surrendered the ether of his youth (his own autobiographical account), resisted his own revolution's successor for thirty years (EPR, 1935), and even disowned his own cosmological constant after Friedman (1922) showed the universe moves — "my greatest blunder," as reported by Gamow.
- **P8 (S):** General relativity delivered modern cosmology (Friedman 1922; Lemaître 1927; Hubble 1929), black holes (Schwarzschild 1916; the singularity theorems 1965–70; the Event Horizon Telescope image, 2019), and gravitational-wave astronomy (LIGO, 2015) — a still-accelerating harvest.
- **P9 (S):** It closed Newton's 228-year wound (1915) and immediately opened the deeper one — the black hole interior, where it wars with quantum theory from Hawking radiation (1974) and the information paradox (1976) to the present.

### T10 · Thermodynamics

- **P1 (S):** It made two things unthinkable at once — the conserved heat-fluid (deleted by Joule's 1843–49 equivalents and the Clausius–Thomson syntheses of 1850–51) and the perpetual motion machine (which the Paris Academy had merely banned as a waste of time in 1775, and which Kelvin's 1851 statement of the second law made impossible in principle).
- **P2 (S):** Heat, work, and every "force" — mechanics, electricity, magnetism, chemistry — became one currency (Mayer 1842; Joule's equivalents; Helmholtz's *Über die Erhaltung der Kraft*, 1847), turning the fire engine's paradox into an exchange rate.
- **P3 (P):** Joule's mechanical equivalent (his 1849 value, 772 ft-lb per °F-pound of water) was a universal conversion but unit-conventional in retrospect, and the true re-scaling constant, k (S = k log W, on Boltzmann's Vienna tombstone), entered only with the statistical consolidation — and was first evaluated by Planck (1901), at the theory's quantum frontier.
- **P4 (P):** Carnot's 26-year-old theorem (1824) plus Joule's fresh 1843–49 experiments fed Clausius (1850) and Thomson (1851), and the promissory notes were honored across 55–125 years (Nernst's heat theorem, 1906; Szilard's demon analysis, 1929; Shannon, 1948; black-hole entropy, Bekenstein 1972 and Hawking 1975) — but the founding leaned on brand-new measurement, not on old data alone.
- **P5 (S):** It demoted caloric to a limiting case rather than refuting its practice: Carnot's theorem, Black's calorimetry, Regnault's data, and even Kelvin's 1848 absolute scale (derived from Carnot's caloric axiom) survived, and in reversible adiabatic change the entropy behaves exactly as the conserved caloric was said to.
- **P6 (S):** Carnot's cycle-mathematics was held in caloric meaning for twenty-six years before Clausius (1850) kept the mathematics and deleted the substance — and S = k log W was re-meant a second time a century on (Szilard 1929; Shannon 1948): the same mathematics made to say a new thing about the world.
- **P7 (P):** The makers paid real but modest coins — Thomson staked his 1848 absolute scale on Carnot's caloric and struggled from his 1847 Oxford meeting with Joule until the 1851 recantation, and Carnot recanted only privately (notes published 1878) — but no founder surrendered a lifelong identity-commitment for the theory.
- **P8 (S):** The harvest never stopped — Gibbs's 1876–78 heterogeneous-equilibrium papers founding physical chemistry; Nernst's 1906 third law structuring low-temperature physics (Kamerlingh Onnes's helium liquefaction 1908, superconductivity 1911); quantum statistics (1924–26); information theory (1948); black-hole thermodynamics (1972–75).
- **P9 (S):** It closed the Carnot–Joule contradiction and instantly opened the irreversibility problem — Loschmidt's reversibility objection (1876) and Zermelo's recurrence objection (1896) against Boltzmann, with Maxwell's 1867 demon generating the information question resolved only by Landauer (1961) — the arrow of time still open a century and a half later.

## PART 3 — POINT NOTES

**P1 — the timing of "at birth."** The definition demands demolition *at birth*, but deletions spread over decades. Germ theory (T3) exposed it: the swan-neck experiments that made spontaneous generation unthinkable (1861) belong to the fermentation-and-generation front, fifteen years before the disease etiology proper (Koch, 1876–84), and the deletion of miasma was not complete until 1884. Thermodynamics (T10) exposed a second variant: its deletion of perpetual motion was sealed by a law's *statement* (Kelvin, 1851), not by an experiment. I read "at birth" as "at the founding program's decisive demonstrations," but the definition does not fix this.

**P2 — postulated-substance unification vs. structural identity.** Caloric (T1) and phlogiston (T7) forced the split: both unified many phenomena under one postulated substance ("everything is X"), which is not the same as revealing that two thought-separate things are one (apple/Moon; light = EM). I coded both P for this reason. The ether (T4) then sharpened it from the other side: the light=EM unification (Maxwell 1865) is of the profound type and was achieved *inside* the ether program, but the postulated unifier — the medium — proved superfluous: unification real, unifier artifactual. P again. The definition does not say which unification type earns the S.

**P3 — physics-shaped and scope-sensitive.** Germ theory (T3) fails this point regardless of depth; the point cannot register non-physical profundity at all. Thermodynamics (T10) exposed the fine line twice: Joule's J is universal but, in retrospect, a unit conversion; k entered via the statistical consolidation and was first evaluated numerically by Planck (1901) — inside T10's scope "to about 1900" only by the skin of the December 1900 paper that belongs to T8. Whether late-but-in-scope constants count, and whether conversion constants count, is under-determined.

**P5/P6 — attribution when the successor keeps the mathematics and deletes the ontology.** The same historical episode does double duty in this instrument: Lorentz→Einstein is T9's P6 = S but only T4's P5 = P and P6 = P; Carnot→Clausius is T10's P5 = S and P6 = S but only T1's P5 = P and P6 = P. I adopted the rule that the points credit the theory that *performs* the embedding or the re-meaning, not the theory that receives it — but the definitions do not settle this, and a coder who inverted the rule would raise T1 and T4 without touching T9 and T10.

**P7 — coin type and timing.** The definition means belief-coin paid by the makers at the founding (Newton, Planck, Einstein). T3 and T5 exposed the wrong-direction cases: coin paid by forerunners against the incumbent doctrine (Semmelweis, dismissed from Vienna and dead by 1865; Snow, rejected by the 1855 Board of Health report) or by endgame defenders (Pettenkofer's 1892 self-ingestion; Priestley's 1796 phlogiston defense). T4 and T10 set the S/P boundary problem: Maxwell's abandonment of his own 1861–62 vortex mechanism (1865) and Thomson's 1847–51 struggle over Carnot's caloric are real, documented surrenders — but neither is identity-level like Newton's action at a distance. I coded them P, reserving S for lifelong-commitment surrenders; the definition leaves the threshold unstated.

**P8 — harvest attribution and the "then D" boundary.** Miasma theory (T5) exposed attribution hardest: the sanitary program's mortality returns were real and were directed by a false etiology (Nightingale's army sanitation, 1855 data). I credited the generative phase (S then D) because the theory's own doctrine guided the interventions, but the counter-reading — the harvest belongs to sanitary engineering, not to miasma, so the theory was degenerating all along — is defensible. The epicycle tradition (T2) exposed the scope problem: its panel scope ends at 1600, before the tradition's actual collapse (1610s–1630s), so the "then D" half had to rest on within-scope evidence (Copernicus's 34 circles without accuracy gains, c. 1510; Tycho's 1572 nova and 1577 comet). How much degeneration must fall inside scope is not specified.

**P9 — deeper floor vs. deferred question vs. fatal anomaly.** The epicycle tradition (T2) exposed the distinction: the *Almagest* re-founded its subject, but the question it left open (the equant's physicality) reads as an internal blemish, while the genuinely deep question (what physically moves the planets) was *assigned to another discipline* (Aristotelian cosmology) — deferral, not opening. Caloric (T1) exposed the timing variant: the conservation-vs-conversion tension surfaced only with Carnot in 1824, thirty-five years after the theory's birth, and functioned as a fatal anomaly rather than as a floor handed over. I coded both P; a more generous reading of "re-founds the subject" would give the *Almagest* the S.
