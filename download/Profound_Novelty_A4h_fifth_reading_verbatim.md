* **P7 (Founders' resistance) — Resistance during birth vs. resistance to successors:**
  The definition groups two distinct psychological phenomena: (a) a founder surrendering their *own* deeply held aesthetic/philosophical commitment to birth their theory (Planck surrendering continuity in 1900; Newton surrendering contact mechanics; Darwin surrendering natural theology), and (b) a founder subsequently resisting the *next* revolution that supersedes them (Einstein resisting quantum indeterminism).
  *Case exposing the ambiguity:* **The Luminiferous Ether (T6) via Lorentz**. Hendrik Lorentz spent his later life resisting Einstein's rejection of absolute time and the ether, expressing deep nostalgia for classical simultaneity. But Lorentz did not pay in his own coin to *create* the ether theory; he resisted its demise. The definition's blending of "costs its makers something they actually held" with "spent thirty years resisting his own revolution's successor" required careful separation: founders who only resisted successors without sacrificing their own prior beliefs to build their theory were coded `F`.

* **P9 (Crisis-chaining) — Fertile foundational opening vs. degenerating anomaly:**
  The definition states that a profound theory "does not finish a subject... and hands its successors a deeper floor to build on." Every scientific theory encounters anomalies. The difficulty lies in differentiating between an anomaly that merely exposes a theory as wrong versus a generative wound that establishes a fertile, higher-order research tradition.
  *Case exposing the ambiguity:* **Ptolemy’s equant (T3) vs. Newton’s action-at-a-distance (T8)**. Ptolemy’s equant was seen as an unphysical, scandalous violation of uniform circular motion for 1,400 years, directly provoking Copernicus. Was the equant an open foundational crisis that "handed successors a deeper floor" (`P`/`S`), or merely an ad hoc patch within an increasingly degenerating tradition? It was coded `P` because while it provoked Copernicus, it acted as a technical roadblock rather than a fertile, positive foundational floor like the quantum measurement problem or gravitational singularities.
