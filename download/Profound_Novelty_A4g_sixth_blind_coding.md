## PART 2 — EVIDENCE LINES

## T1 · The caloric theory of heat

- **P1 — F:** Lavoisier's 1789 *Traité élémentaire de chimie* *added* "calorique" to the table of elements (with his own hedge that it might not be matter), and the kinetic alternative (Bernoulli's 1738 *Hydrodynamica*, Rumford's 1798 cannon-boring, Davy's 1799 ice-rubbing) remained thinkable and sayable throughout — nothing became unthinkable.
- **P2 — F:** Caloric multiplied distinctions (Black's latent vs. free caloric, c. 1761; self-repulsion to explain gas pressure) rather than collapsing any, and the true unification of heat with motion arrived only with its defeat — Joule (1843–49) and Helmholtz's 1847 *Erhaltung der Kraft*.
- **P3 — F:** The program's numbers were substance parameters (Black's latent heats; Regnault's 1840s–50s specific heats), and the one constant that re-scaled physics — the mechanical equivalent J, ~4.2 J/cal — was installed *against* caloric by Joule, not by it.
- **P4 — P:** Carnot's 1824 *Réflexions* derived the maximum-efficiency theorem with no new experiments, and that theorem was honored decades later when Clausius and Thomson rebuilt it as the second law (1850–51) — but the framework's wider career was data-fitted (Regnault's thermometry) and it was killed by *new* data (Joule's paddle wheels), the reverse of derivation-first robustness.
- **P5 — F:** Caloric accumulated on Black's calorimetry as data rather than defeating any successful predecessor theory, and it never gave the rival kinetic conception (Bernoulli 1738) a limiting case — it simply opposed it, and was itself refuted outright (Rumford 1798; Davy 1799; fatally, Joule 1843–49) rather than demoted.
- **P6 — P:** Fourier's 1822 *Théorie analytique de la chaleur* kept the conduction mathematics deliberately indifferent to caloric ontology, and Carnot's efficiency theorem outlived its substance reading (Clausius 1850) — formalism and meaning did come apart, but the separation was exploited by the theory's successors, not by it.
- **P7 — F:** No founder paid a coin to install caloric: Lavoisier hedged it into the element table as provisional, and Carnot's posthumous notes (published by his brother Hippolyte in 1878) show him privately abandoning conservation of caloric for conversion — recantation, not payment.
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative — Fourier's 1822 heat equation, Carnot's 1824 theorem (seed of the second law), Regnault's gas data feeding the ideal-gas law; degenerative — by the 1840s–50s the program survived only by multiplying internal structure, and after Joule's 1843–49 experiments Hirn's 1850s–60s engine measurements fought a losing rearguard that the Clausius–Thomson syntheses of 1850–51 formally closed out.
- **P9 — P:** Caloric closed Black's latent-heat anomaly crisis (1761) and its Carnot core opened the work-value-of-heat question, but the substance itself left no live deeper floor — the entropy its successors built was re-read *out of* Carnot (Clausius 1865), not bequeathed by caloric.

## T2 · Darwinian evolution by natural selection

- **P1 — S:** The *Origin* (November 1859) deleted the fixity of species and the design argument as scientific explanations — the special-creation question died in science within a generation (the June 1860 Oxford Huxley–Wilberforce debate marks the turn) — and the *Descent of Man* (1871) deleted the separate creation of humans ("man still bears in his bodily frame the indelible stamp of his lowly origin").
- **P2 — S:** Common descent revealed the boundaries among classification, embryology, and biogeography as artifacts of description: John Gould's identification of the Galápagos mockingbirds as distinct species (March 1837) provoked Darwin's first branching sketch (Notebook B, summer 1837), making the "natural system," vestigial organs, and the fossil record one genealogical fact.
- **P3 — F:** Neither the *Origin* nor the *Descent* installs any universal constant — the theory works with rates and populations, and its quantitative structure (Fisher, 1930) belongs to the explicitly excluded synthesis.
- **P4 — S:** The theory was derived from old material — Malthus's 1798 *Essay* (read by Darwin in September 1838), Lyell's *Principles*, the Beagle specimens — and its promissory notes were honored: *Archaeopteryx* turned up in 1861, two years after the *Origin*, and Darwin's 1862 prediction of a pollinator for *Angraecum sesquipedale* was confirmed by Rothschild and Jordan's *Xanthopan morganii praedicta* (1903).
- **P5 — P:** Darwin explained why fixity had *seemed* true (the imperfection of the geological record, *Origin* ch. IX) and why Paley's adaptations were real observations needing a mechanism, but design-as-explanation was refuted and displaced rather than derived as a limiting case, and the quantitative embedding came only with the excluded synthesis.
- **P6 — F:** The *Origin* contains no formalism whose meaning could come apart — its one quantitative claim (geometric vs. arithmetic increase) is borrowed Malthusian arithmetic; the mathematical theory of selection is precisely the excluded post-1918 material.
- **P7 — S:** Darwin paid in his own coin: "I do not think I hardly ever admired a book more than Paley's *Natural Theology*" (*Autobiography*, 1876), yet he had to abandon that framework, kept his 1844 essay sealed with instructions to Emma for fear of the reception, and endured Sedgwick's 1860 letter ("I have read your book with more pain than pleasure… parts I laughed at till my sides were almost sore").
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative through 1859–71 (six *Origin* editions, orchid pollination 1862, sexual selection in the *Descent* 1871, Haeckel's *Generelle Morphologie* 1866), but the selectionist core strained after Fleeming Jenkin's 1867 *North British Review* swamping critique exposed blending heredity's fatal math, and degenerated into the eclipse of the 1880s–90s (Cope's and Eimer's neo-Lamarckism; de Vries's *Mutationstheorie*, 1901–03) before the excluded synthesis revived it.
- **P9 — S:** Darwin closed the fixity crisis and opened a deeper floor: the mechanism of heredity — Jenkin's 1867 objection and the failed pangenesis of *Variation* (1868) left inheritance open until the 1900 rediscovery of Mendel, while Kelvin's 1860s Earth-age estimates (20–400 Myr) attacked the very timetable gradualism required.

## T3 · The epicycle tradition

- **P1 — F:** The *Almagest* (c. 150 CE) accumulated devices — deferents, epicycles, eccentrics, the equant — added planet by planet to fit observations, preserving Aristotle's two-worlds cosmology intact; the tradition's whole career is incremental parameter adjustment.
- **P2 — P:** The Apollonian equivalence (an eccentric hypothesis is geometrically identical to an epicycle on a concentric, deployed in *Almagest* III) showed that a choice of description was no joint in nature, and one mathematical toolkit served Sun, Moon, and all five planets (*Almagest* III–XIII) — but the unity stayed formal, and Sun vs. Earth, celestial vs. terrestrial, remained separate in kind.
- **P3 — F:** The system's quantities were per-planet parameters — mean motions, epicycle ratios, apogees, fitted to the Babylonian eclipse records Hipparchus had used — with nothing universal in the G/c/h sense anywhere in the tradition through Tycho.
- **P4 — F:** This was curve-fitting, not derivation: parameters were adjusted to observed positions (Hipparchus's solar theory built on Chaldean eclipse cycles; each equant placed to fit), and the tradition issued ephemeris service (the Alfonsine Tables' eclipses) rather than promissory notes of a new conceptual order.
- **P5 — F:** The tradition replaced the Eudoxan homocentric spheres (which failed on planetary brightness variation) without deriving them as any limiting case, merely dovetailing its models with Aristotelian spheres in the *Planetary Hypotheses* — replacement and coexistence, not embedding.
- **P6 — P:** The tradition institutionalized the formalism/meaning split — Simplicius (*In De Caelo*, reporting Geminus) states that astronomers need only "save the phenomena" with any geometry, leaving physical truth to the physicists — but no re-meaning ever transformed the world-picture from *inside*; the re-meaning that mattered (same apparatus, Sun-centered, 1543) came from outside.
- **P7 — F:** No founder is recorded as surrendering anything held: Ptolemy credits Hipparchus and builds; the Islamic continuation (al-Battani's *Zij* c. 900; al-Tusi's couple, 1247) adds and refines at no personal conceptual cost.
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative for a long phase — the *Handy Tables*, the *zij* tradition (al-Battani c. 900; the Toledan Tables 1080; the Alfonsine Tables c. 1272 serving Europe to the 1550s; Ulugh Beg's Samarkand catalogue 1437; Ibn al-Shatir's models c. 1370, later shown by Swerdlow and Kennedy to be mathematically identical to Copernicus's) — then degenerating: by the 1570s–80s Tycho's instruments exposed the tables' errors and his nova (1572) and comet (1577) shattered the solid spheres the tradition presupposed.
- **P9 — P:** The tradition closed the ancient prediction problem (reliable ephemerides) and left a standing wound — the physical meaning of its own devices, quarantined by the saving-the-phenomena doctrine — but that was a deficit it never metabolized into a deeper program; the deeper floor was laid against it by Kepler (1609) and Newton (1687).

## T4 · The fixity of species

- **P1 — F:** The framework entrenched immutability (Ray's *Wisdom of God*, 1691; Linnaeus's *Systema Naturae*, 1735) and merely suppressed transformist speculation — Lamarck's *Philosophie zoologique* (1809) remained entirely thinkable, just unpopular.
- **P2 — F:** Linnaeus's sexual system unified nomenclature and natural theology organized adaptation as design, but the framework's ontology multiplied created kinds; no distinction thought separate was revealed as an artifact of description.
- **P3 — F:** No constant: the framework's quantitative life (Linnaean counts, Cuvier's stratigraphy) contained nothing analogous to G, c, or h, and none was ever proposed.
- **P4 — F:** Adjustment, not derivation: Cuvier's 1796 proof of extinction (the mammoth and mastodon papers to the Institut) forced the ad hoc postulate of catastrophes, and Agassiz's *Essay on Classification* (1857) multiplied successive creations to absorb the fossil succession — misfits generated additions, never promissory notes.
- **P5 — F:** The framework never defeated a successful rival honestly — it absorbed Aristotelian essences and Genesis by synthesis, and when evolution arrived it was met with denunciation (the 1860 Oxford debate's tone) rather than any derivation of where the old view worked and must fail.
- **P6 — F:** No formalism existed whose meaning could come apart — Paley's *Natural Theology* (1802) is an argument from contrivance, not mathematics; there were no formulas to hold fixed while changing what they meant.
- **P7 — F:** Its builders paid nothing they held — Ray, Linnaeus, Cuvier, and Paley kept or enhanced their standing within it; the coin was paid by the challengers (Darwin's twenty-year delay), not by the framework's founders.
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative in its phase — the framework sponsored the descriptive sciences: Ray's *Historia Plantarum* (1686–1704), Linnaeus's *Systema Naturae* (1735), Cuvier's *Recherches sur les ossemens fossiles* (1812), comparative anatomy and palaeontology founded under fixist catastrophism; degenerating by the 1850s — Owen's "law of the succession of types" was uncomfortably directional, and Agassiz's 1857 *Essay* met it with ever more multiple creations.
- **P9 — F:** Design was a terminus, not a re-foundation: Paley's watchmaker (1802) closes explanation in a person; the framework suppressed the succession-of-types question it could not metabolize and handed successors no deeper floor — only a data base bequeathed as anomaly.

## T5 · Germ theory of disease

- **P1 — S:** The doctrine made two things unthinkable — spontaneous generation, after Pasteur's swan-neck flask experiments (1861) and Tyndall's 1877 spore work closed every remaining refuge, and miasma etiology, after Koch's comma bacillus (1883–84) and the 1892 Hamburg–Altona epidemic contrast (unfiltered water stricken, filtered water spared) settled the question against foul air.
- **P2 — S:** Germ theory dissolved the ancient contagion-vs-miasma antinomy — the deadlock from Fracastoro's *De contagione* (1546) through the International Sanitary Conferences of 1851–83 — by showing one agent spreading both by contact and through the environment (the comma bacillus living in water), after Pasteur's 1857–60 fermentation memoirs had already united the chemists' territory (against Liebig's catalytic theory) with life and extended it to silkworm disease (1865), anthrax (Pouilly-le-Fort, 1881), and rabies (1885).
- **P3 — F:** No universal constant was installed: the program's quantities were biological parameters — incubation periods, thermal death points — and Koch's postulates (1882 lecture; 1890 formulation) are methodological criteria, not constants of nature.
- **P4 — P:** The theory retro-explained old observations at a stroke (Holmes's 1843 and Semmelweis's 1847 puerperal-fever evidence; Snow's 1854 Broad Street and Southwark–Lambeth analyses) — over-determined by old data — but its consolidation was experiment-first at every step (Koch's anthrax 1876, tuberculosis 1882, cholera 1883–84), the opposite of Newton's economy with new observations.
- **P5 — P:** Germ theory explained why the miasma-driven sanitary program had worked (sewers and clean water remove the vibrio's vehicles) and where it failed (Snow's 1854 Southwark–Lambeth water-companies comparison: same air, different water), but it refuted the miasma ontology outright rather than recovering it in any limit — inheriting the practices without embedding the theory.
- **P6 — F:** The doctrine had no formalism whose meaning could come apart — its canon is procedural (Koch's postulates; Pasteur's experimental reports), so the Lorentz/Schrödinger pattern of same-equation-new-world could not arise.
- **P7 — P:** The founders' payments were methodological rather than metaphysical: Koch had to weaken his own etiological standard for cholera (the 1884 report, lacking an animal model, substituted pathological anatomy for the inoculation criterion — which his Munich rivals threw back at him), and Pasteur endured Liebig's attack on vital fermentation, but neither surrendered a deep personal commitment the way Newton abandoned the mechanical philosophy.
- **P8 — S:** The harvest never stopped ordering itself — Lister's antiseptic surgery (1865–67, applied Pasteur directly), Behring and Kitasato's diphtheria antitoxin (1890, first Nobel Prize in Medicine, 1901), Yersin's plague bacillus (1894), the Reed Commission's verification of Finlay's mosquito-vector yellow-fever theory (1900–01), and onward to *H. pylori* (Marshall and Warren, 1983–84) — a century and a half of un-ordered consequences.
- **P9 — S:** Germ theory closed the epidemic-cause crisis and opened deeper floors: virology (Ivanovsky's 1892 filtration; Beijerinck's 1898 "contagium vivum fluidum" — agents its own Koch standards could not see), immunology (Metchnikoff's phagocytes 1884; Ehrlich's side-chains 1897, running to clonal selection 1957), and the carrier state ("Typhoid Mary" Mallon, 1907, straining Koch's postulates themselves).

## T6 · The luminiferous ether

- **P1 — P:** The wave-ether program did delete Newton's light-corpuscles — Poisson derived the bright-spot *reductio* from Fresnel's 1819 prize memoir and Arago observed it, and Foucault's 1850 rotating-mirror measurement (light slower in water) finished the emission theory — but the ether itself was a *substance added* (Huygens's *Traité de la Lumière*, written 1678, published 1690): ontology accumulated rather than deleted.
- **P2 — S:** The ether carried the century's greatest unification: Maxwell's "A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field" (1865) identified light with transverse electromagnetic waves via the Weber–Kohlrausch ratio (1856), making optics and electromagnetism one subject — the thought-to-be-separate revealed as one phenomenon in one medium.
- **P3 — P:** The program *determined the value* of c (Fizeau's 1849 toothed-wheel measurement; Maxwell's derivation from the 1856 Weber–Kohlrausch ratio) but never *installed it as universal*: within the ether program c was the wave speed of a medium in a preferred frame, kept frame-relative by Fresnel's dragging coefficient (confirmed by Fizeau's 1851 water-tube experiment) — its universality is Einstein's 1905 act, not the ether's.
- **P4 — P:** Maxwell's synthesis was genuinely derivation-first (the wave speed fell out of old electrical ratios and matched Fizeau's 1849 number; Hertz's 1887 Karlsruhe experiments honored the promissory note twenty-two years later), but the theory's defining posit — the medium — was never derived from anything and after the 1887 Michelson–Morley null survived only through ad hoc rescues (FitzGerald's 1889 contraction; Lorentz's 1892–95 versions).
- **P5 — P:** The program conservatively embedded Newtonian ray optics as the short-wavelength limit — explaining why rectilinear propagation, reflection, and refraction had worked, and where they fail (diffraction at apertures comparable to λ, the Poisson–Arago spot) — but the corpuscular theory it defeated (Foucault 1850) it refuted outright, and its own medium received no limiting case from its successor.
- **P6 — S:** The canonical case: Lorentz held the full mathematics by 1904 (the transformations, local time, even relativistic velocity addition, in "Electromagnetic phenomena in a system moving with any velocity smaller than that of light") with the ether meaning — real FitzGerald contraction of matter moving through the medium — and Einstein's June 1905 paper made the same equations say *frame change*, deleting the medium; the profundity lived entirely in the reading.
- **P7 — F:** The ether demanded no coin of its makers — it was the comfortable ontology of the age, and Kelvin's April 1900 Royal Institution lecture "Nineteenth-Century Clouds" shows the establishment treating the Michelson–Morley null as a patchable annoyance *within* the program; even Lorentz, after accepting the equations' Einsteinian content, retained the ether as a preferred frame into the 1920s.
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative — Fresnel's wave optics (the 1819 prize memoir, polarization, crystal optics), Maxwell's 1865 field theory, Hertz's 1887 waves opening radio — then degenerative after 1887: the null was met with the FitzGerald (1889) and Lorentz (1892) contraction patches, and after 1905 the medium offered no new prediction at all, dying as untestable surplus.
- **P9 — P:** The ether closed the propagation question (Huygens 1690 gave light a medium) and its own ether-wind requirement opened the crisis that produced special relativity (Michelson–Morley 1887), but it bequeathed no deeper floor of its own — its formal legacy passed to successors through Lorentz's equations while its ontology simply died.

## T7 · Miasma theory

- **P1 — F:** Miasma theory added an etiological agent (putrescent exhalations) and made nothing unthinkable — it was itself the thing deleted; its vocabulary survived only as folk residue ("mal-aria") after the 1880s.
- **P2 — F:** The theory lumped distinct etiologies under one cause — Farr's 1852 Registrar-General's cholera regression on elevation treated Thames-side air as the variable, while Snow's 1854 Southwark–Lambeth analysis showed the water doing the work — a false generalization, not a revealed identity.
- **P3 — F:** No constant was proposed or installed anywhere in the airs-waters-places tradition through the sanitary era.
- **P4 — F:** The theory accommodated rather than derived: every epidemic was absorbed by local miasmas or "epidemic constitutions," and its strongest quantitative exercise (Farr's 1852 elevation–mortality law) was a fitted correlation that could not metabolize Snow's 1855 water-companies data.
- **P5 — F:** Miasma was displaced, not demoted: Koch's comma bacillus (1883–84) and the Hamburg–Altona contrast of 1892 refuted the filth-exhalation core outright, with no limiting case recovered — the surviving sanitary practices were inherited under a new rationale, which is the successor's practice-preservation, not theory-embedding.
- **P6 — F:** There was no formalism to re-mean — the theory lived in qualitative environmental description from the Hippocratic *Airs, Waters, Places* to Chadwick's 1842 *Report*.
- **P7 — F:** Its champions paid nothing they held: Chadwick suffered politically (pushed out amid the General Board of Health's demise in 1854–58), but no leading miasmatist surrendered a scientific commitment to install the doctrine — it was the default inheritance, not a costly novelty.
- **P8 — S then D:** Real harvest, wrong theory: the sanitary movement it inspired produced the 1848 Public Health Act and Bazalgette's London main drainage (1859–65, forced by the Great Stink of 1858), and the cholera the sewers inadvertently helped end (London's last epidemic, 1866) was a consequence nobody had ordered; degenerating from the 1880s, when Pettenkofer's ground-water theory made Munich the program's bastion and his October 1892 self-ingestion of Koch's cholera culture was its last coin — a demonstration that obstructed rather than generated.
- **P9 — F:** The theory was a terminus: it answered "why filth and disease correlate" with an ancient formula and opened no deeper floor — when it fell (Koch 1883–84; Hamburg–Altona 1892), it handed successors nothing but the infrastructure it had never theoretically earned.

## T8 · Newtonian mechanics

- **P1 — S:** The *Principia* (1687) deleted the two-worlds cosmos — the Moon test of Book III showed the Moon falling toward Earth exactly as the apple does (centripetal acceleration 1/3600 g at sixty Earth radii), abolishing the celestial–terrestrial distinction of Aristotle's *De Caelo* that had organized cosmology for two millennia.
- **P2 — S:** Universal gravitation unified what all prior astronomy held apart: the apple and the Moon, the planets and the tides, the comets and the precession of the equinoxes became one force's work (Book III), with even Halley's comet (1682) promised the same law as Jupiter's moons.
- **P3 — S:** G was installed as the universal coupling of matter to matter (Book III, Props. 7–8), blind to what the matter is, with its numerical value requiring only measurement — supplied by Cavendish's 1798 torsion-balance "weighing of the Earth."
- **P4 — S:** Newton derived his system from old data — Kepler's laws (from the Rudolphine Tables, 1627) and Huygens's 1673 *Horologium Oscillatorium* — and issued promissory notes honored for a century: Halley's comet returned on schedule (observed by Palitzsch, Christmas 1758; perihelion March 1759 after Clairaut's recalculation), Maupertuis's 1736–37 Lapland arc confirmed the predicted oblateness, and Neptune was found on paper (Le Verrier; Galle and d'Arrest, September 23, 1846).
- **P5 — S:** The *Principia* conservatively embedded its successful predecessors: Kepler's area law and ellipse are derived (Book I, Props. 1–2, 11) and the 3/2 law (Book I, Prop. 15), with their failure modes predicted as perturbations — the lunar inequalities and the Jupiter–Saturn great inequality that Laplace resolved (1785–86) exactly where the two-body idealization broke down.
- **P6 — P:** Newton built the split into his own formalism — "hypotheses non fingo" (General Scholium, 2nd edition, 1713) left the mathematics of attraction officially stripped of physical meaning, and the second law's own meaning shifted between Newton's impulse form and the later F = ma reading (as I. B. Cohen's analysis of the *Principia* shows) — but the profundity never consisted in a re-meaning of a held formalism; Leibniz held the calculus, not the physics.
- **P7 — S:** Newton paid exactly in his own coin, surrendering the mechanical philosophy's ban on action at a distance and being attacked as an occultist for it — Huygens's 1690 letter to Leibniz refusing attraction as a principle, and Leibniz's charge in the Leibniz–Clarke correspondence (1715–16) that attraction was "an occult quality," a "perpetual miracle."
- **P8 — S:** The harvest is the history of exact science: Euler's and Clairaut's lunar theories, Laplace's *Mécanique céleste* (1799–1825), the 1758 comet return, Neptune in 1846, perturbation theory as a discipline — a century and more of consequences nobody had ordered.
- **P9 — S:** The General Scholium (1713) left the mechanism of gravity explicitly unexplained — the wound Leibniz's "occult" charge kept open — and it stayed open for 228 years until Einstein's November 1915 field equations closed it, while the three-body problem the *Principia* handed down became Poincaré's chaos (1889–99): a re-founded subject with a deeper floor.

## T9 · Phlogiston chemistry

- **P1 — F:** Phlogiston was an ontological addition — Becher's *terra pinguis* (1669) renamed and generalized by Stahl (from 1703) into the principle of inflammability — and nothing previously thinkable became unthinkable under its reign.
- **P2 — P:** The framework genuinely unified combustion, calcination, and respiration as one process class (phlogiston's exit), and its air-economy insight — vegetation restoring to the air what fire and animals consume — gestured at a real biogeochemical unity; but the unification rested on reifying a common substance, not on revealing a distinction as an artifact of description.
- **P3 — F:** No constant: the theory's numbers were weights and assays, and its central quantity had a sign problem — metals *gain* weight on calcination (observed since Jean Rey, 1630) — that no G/c/h-type constant could have redeemed.
- **P4 — F:** The theory issued no honored promissory notes: it accommodated every result with elastic phlogiston (lighter-than-air variants floated in the 1770s–80s to save the calx-weight anomaly), and Lavoisier's 1783 "Réflexions sur le phlogistique" nailed the mode — phlogiston as "a veritable Proteus" changing form to fit each case.
- **P5 — F:** Phlogiston was refuted outright, not demoted: Lavoisier's sealed-vessel calcinations (1770s) and the *Traité* (1789) replaced the substance with oxygen chemistry, and the phlogistonians' response (Kirwan's 1787 *Essay on Phlogiston*, translated 1788 with the Lavoisians' rebuttals appended) was defense, not any derivation of where the old theory worked and must fail.
- **P6 — F:** The theory was operational and qualitative (Stahl's *Fundamenta Chymiae*, 1723) — there was no formalism whose meaning could come apart; its successor replaced the substance-talk wholesale rather than re-reading a held equation.
- **P7 — F:** Its founders paid nothing they held — Becher and Stahl enlarged their inheritance at no conceptual cost — and its most famous defender paid in the *other* direction: Priestley's 1796 pamphlet "The Doctrine of Phlogiston Established" defended it until his death in Northumberland, Pennsylvania (1804), against the new chemistry he refused.
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative — the pneumatic revolution happened entirely inside phlogiston chemistry: Black's fixed air (1754), Cavendish's inflammable air (1766), Priestley's and Scheele's dephlogisticated/fire air (1771–75) — the discovery of oxygen itself was a phlogiston-guided achievement; degenerating from the mid-1780s, when Cavendish's water synthesis (1784) and Lavoisier's balance-sheets forced the Protean adjustments that Kirwan's 1787 defense exemplified.
- **P9 — P:** Phlogiston closed the "what is combustion" question with a unifying answer, and its practice handed successors the airs (the pneumatic program was the real floor), but its standing bequest — the calx-weight anomaly running from Rey (1630) onward — was a defect it could not metabolize, not a deeper problem opened by its success.

## T10 · Quantum mechanics

- **P1 — S:** Quantum theory deleted determinism and the classical trajectory itself: Heisenberg's uncertainty paper (1927) made ΔxΔp ≳ ħ a matter of principle rather than measurement error, and the Born rule (1926) made individual events irreducibly chancy — what Schrödinger's cat (1935) dramatized and Bohr's Como lecture (1927) systematized.
- **P2 — S:** Wave and particle unified: de Broglie's 1924 thesis assigned λ = h/p to matter, confirmed by Davisson–Germer electron diffraction (1927), and Born's reading made one formal object (ψ) carry both the wave's interference and the particle's discrete detections.
- **P3 — S:** h was installed by Planck on December 14, 1900 (E = hν, from the Rubens–Kurlbaum infrared data) and re-scaled the whole conceptual system — action itself quantized — becoming the third load-bearing constant.
- **P4 — S:** Over-determined by old data, under-determined by old theory: Bohr's 1913 atom was derived from Balmer's 1885 formula plus Rutherford's 1911 nucleus with no new experiment, and the promissory notes were honored on schedule — de Broglie's 1924 matter waves verified by Davisson–Germer (1927), Dirac's 1928 equation paying off in Anderson's positron (1932).
- **P5 — S:** The classical world was recovered as a limit: Bohr's correspondence principle (1913–18) built the theory on its own classical limit, and Ehrenfest's theorem (1927) derived Newton's equations for expectation values — large quantum numbers give back the old mechanics, which is why it had worked.
- **P6 — S:** The canonical split: Schrödinger held the equation (1926) with a real-wave meaning, and Born's collision papers (June–July 1926, the probabilistic |ψ|² reading inserted in proof as a footnote) made the same mathematics say probability — the profundity was an interpretation, over Schrödinger's recorded objections to "all this damned quantum jumping."
- **P7 — S:** The founders paid in their own coin: Planck called his 1900 hypothesis "an act of desperation" (letter to R. W. Wood, 1931) and tried to confine quantization to emission in his 1911 second theory; Schrödinger regretted the revolution he had authored (his reported 1926 remark to Bohr); and Einstein declined the next coin — determinism — from the December 4, 1926 letter to Born ("He does not play dice") through EPR (1935), thirty years of resistance.
- **P8 — S:** The century of harvest: the transistor (Bardeen–Brattain, 1947), the laser, the periodic table and chemistry's foundations, Bell's 1964 inequality and Aspect's 1981–82 tests, quantum information (Feynman 1982; Shor 1994) — the program still orders unrequested consequences a hundred years on.
- **P9 — S:** The theory closed the blackbody and atomic-stability crises and opened the measurement problem — open for a century and counting (EPR 1935; Schrödinger's cat 1935; Bell 1964) — plus its war with general relativity over the black-hole interior: a re-founded physics with a deeper floor.

## T11 · Relativity

- **P1 — S:** Special relativity deleted the ether and absolute simultaneity in one stroke (the 1905 paper's kinematic part: no preferred frame, simultaneity frame-relative), and general relativity (1915) deleted absolute space and time altogether, making geometry dynamical.
- **P2 — S:** Space and time unified (Minkowski's 1908 Cologne lecture: "henceforth space by itself and time by itself are doomed to fade away into mere shadows"), mass and energy unified (E = mc², 1905), gravity and geometry unified (the November 1915 field equations: gravitation as curvature, free fall as geodesic).
- **P3 — S:** c was installed as the universal cap on propagation — the 1905 second postulate making light-speed frame-invariant, re-scaling space, time, and energy into one another — the second load-bearing constant.
- **P4 — S:** Derivation-first on old data: the 1887 Michelson–Morley null (eighteen years old in 1905) and, for general relativity, the Mercury anomaly Le Verrier had tabulated in 1859 (43″/century) — with promissory notes honored across a century: light bending at the 1919 Dyson–Eddington eclipse, gravitational redshift by Pound–Rebka (1959–60), gravitational waves by LIGO (detected September 14, 2015, a hundred years after the 1916 prediction).
- **P5 — S:** Both theories recover their predecessors exactly in limits: special relativity reduces to Galilean kinematics as v/c → 0 (Einstein's own demonstration in the 1905 paper), and general relativity reduces to Newtonian gravity in the weak-field limit — the November 1915 perihelion calculation was performed in precisely that limit, explaining why Newton had worked and where (Mercury) he had to fail.
- **P6 — S:** Lorentz held the mathematics (the 1904 transformations with the real-contraction meaning; Poincaré's 1905 refinements) before Einstein's June 1905 paper made the same equations mean frame change — the profundity was invisible in the formulas and lived entirely in what they were taken to say.
- **P7 — S:** Einstein surrendered the ether of his youth to make 1905 possible — his 1920 Leyden lecture "Ether and the Theory of Relativity" shows how much the deletion cost and how tempting partial reinstatement remained — and then, when the quantum asked him to surrender determinism, declined, paying thirty years of resistance instead (the December 4, 1926 letter to Born; EPR 1935).
- **P8 — S:** The un-ordered harvest: Friedmann (1922) and Lemaître (1927) made cosmology a science, Hubble (1929) and the Penzias–Wilson microwave background (1965) confirmed it, Schwarzschild's 1916 solution became black holes (Cygnus X-1, 1971) and gravitational-wave astronomy (LIGO 2015) — a century of consequences nobody ordered.
- **P9 — S:** The theory closed the ether-drift crisis and opened the deepest floor now standing — the incompatibility with quantum mechanics over the black-hole interior and spacetime's quantum structure — a re-founded subject whose successors are still building on the open wound.

## T12 · Thermodynamics

- **P1 — S:** The syntheses deleted two things at a stroke: the conserved caloric fluid (Clausius's 1850 re-founding of Carnot without conservation of heat) and the very possibility of perpetual motion of the second kind (Kelvin's 1851 statement: no mechanical effect from cooling a body below the coldest of its surroundings) — whole classes of thinkable machines became unthinkable.
- **P2 — S:** Energy unified what had been separate domains — falling bodies, heat, electricity, chemistry, and life were made one conserved currency (Joule's 1843–49 equivalents; Helmholtz's 1847 *Erhaltung der Kraft*) — and Boltzmann's 1877 work unified macroscopic irreversibility with molecular statistics (S ∝ log W), revealing the law/chance boundary itself as a matter of counting.
- **P3 — P:** Kelvin's 1848 absolute thermometric scale (built on Carnot) created a genuinely new dimensional currency, but its universal conversion factor to energy — k_B — entered physics only at the theory's statistical edge (Planck, December 1900, k = R/N); the core laws of 1850–65 install no G/c/h-type constant.
- **P4 — P:** The second law was derivation-first — Clausius (1850) re-derived Carnot's theorem from an impossibility axiom, needing no new experiment — but the first law's numerical core was measurement-driven (Joule's paddle-wheel series, 1843–49, fixing ~4.2 J/cal), so the theory is only half derivation-first.
- **P5 — S:** The defeat of caloric was the honest kind: Clausius's 1850 paper kept Carnot's theorems intact while deleting the substance premise, and the no-work limit (pure conduction) recovers caloric bookkeeping exactly — Fourier's 1822 conduction mathematics, built on caloric assumptions, survives untouched as the W → 0 limit, which is why caloric's calorimetry had worked.
- **P6 — S:** The same formal quantity received a second meaning: Clausius's entropy (1865, "the transformation content," dS = δQ/T) was re-read by Boltzmann (1877) as S ∝ log W — mechanical probability — after Loschmidt's 1876 reversibility objection and Zermelo's 1896 recurrence objection forced the statistical meaning; the equations stood while the meaning changed from law to likelihood.
- **P7 — S:** The founders paid visibly: Thomson abandoned the caloric-Carnot foundation of his own 1848 absolute-scale paper, conceding in the 1851 "Dynamical Theory of Heat" that Joule's experiments forced the change; and Boltzmann surrendered the strict truth of the second law after Loschmidt (1876), defending the probabilistic concession and atomism against Mach and Ostwald until his death at Duino in 1906.
- **P8 — S:** The harvest runs through modern science: Gibbs's 1876–78 heterogeneous equilibrium (the phase rule, chemistry's foundation), every heat engine and refrigerator from Rankine to Linde, Nernst's 1906 third law, Szilard's 1929 demon and Shannon's 1948 entropy founding information theory, and free-energy biochemistry (Lipmann, 1941).
- **P9 — S:** The theory closed the Carnot–Joule contradiction (resolved 1850–51) and opened deeper floors still occupied: the atomistic foundation (contested by Mach and Ostwald, unresolved at Boltzmann's death in 1906, settled by Einstein's 1905 Brownian motion and Perrin's 1908–09 counts), the arrow-of-time question (why the universe began in a low-entropy state — still open), and the demon-information problem (Szilard 1929 → Landauer 1961).

## T13 · The fixity of the crust

- **P1 — F:** The settlement deleted nothing and accumulated instead: to absorb the southern-hemisphere distributions (the Glossopteris flora across India, Africa, South America, Australia, Antarctica) it multiplied postulated land bridges — Suess's Gondwâna-Land (*Das Antlitz der Erde*, 1885) — rather than making any prior picture unthinkable.
- **P2 — F:** The framework defended the deepest distinction it inherited — continents and ocean basins as primordially different and fixed (Dana's *Manual of Geology* position) — which is precisely why biogeographic disjunctions strained it; it is the antithesis of unification.
- **P3 — F:** No constant: the settlement's quantities were local stratigraphy (Hall's Appalachian thicknesses, 1857) and geosynclinal subsidence histories (Dana's 1873 coinage), never a universal parameter.
- **P4 — F:** No derivation, no promissory notes: the 1926 AAPG Tulsa symposium on continental drift (published 1928) rejected Wegener without offering a predictive success of its own, and the program's answers to anomalies were post hoc bridges, not derived consequences.
- **P5 — F:** The settlement never defeated a successful predecessor honestly — it consolidated Lyellian uniformitarianism and contractionist orogeny (Élie de Beaumont) by accumulation, and when radiogenic heat (Joly, 1903 onward) refuted the cooling-contraction mechanism, nothing was demoted to a limit; the framework simply shed parts.
- **P6 — F:** No formalism existed whose meaning could come apart — geosynclinal theory and land-bridge biogeography were qualitative syntheses, not mathematics, so the Lorentz pattern had no purchase.
- **P7 — F:** The settlement's defenders paid nothing they held: the 1926 symposium participants (the American oil-geology establishment) risked nothing personal by defending fixism, which enhanced their institutional position — the coin was paid by the other side (Wegener died on the Greenland ice in 1930, ridiculed).
- **P8 — S then D:** Generative in its phase: the settlement organized the great surveys (Hall's New York work, 1857; King's 40th-Parallel survey, 1867–77; the USGS, 1879) and geosynclinal theory guided a century of stratigraphic mapping whose data base plate tectonics would later explain; degenerating from the 1920s — each anomaly cost more ad hoc structure (multiplying postulated bridges; the dismissal of Runcorn's 1956 polar-wander curves), the signature of a program paying more than it returned.
- **P9 — F:** The settlement was a terminus: it posed no deeper question (continents fixed — no mechanism to seek), and when it fell (1962–68) it bequeathed its data base but no open problem of its own making — the deep questions were opened by its successor, against it.

## T14 · Plate tectonics

- **P1 — S:** The theory made the fixist picture unthinkable in under a decade: Hess's 1962 "History of Ocean Basins" (the ocean floor young and in motion, offered as "geopoetry"), Vine and Matthews's 1963 reading of the ridge anomalies, and the DSDP Leg 3 drilling (1968–69, South Atlantic sediments aging with distance from the ridge) deleted permanent continents and oceans — and the land-bridge question died with them, since a young Atlantic floor leaves no place for the bridges.
- **P2 — S:** One kinematic system unified what had been separate disciplines: continental geology, marine geophysics, seismology (the Wadati–Benioff zones), and paleomagnetism were made one subject by plate geometry — Wilson's 1965 transform-fault class, McKenzie and Parker's 1967 Euler-pole Pacific solution, Morgan's 1968 global plate set — and even the Appalachians became the closure of Wilson's re-opened proto-Atlantic Iapetus ("Did the Atlantic close and then re-open?", 1966).
- **P3 — F:** No universal constant was installed: the theory's numbers are local parameters — spreading half-rates from ~2 cm/yr in the South Atlantic to ~7–9 cm/yr on the East Pacific Rise — and the Euler-pole machinery is classical spherical geometry (Euler's rotation theorem), not a new constant of nature.
- **P4 — P:** Not derivation-first in Newton's economy — the theory needed the post-1945 oceanographic data flood (Heezen and Tharp's rifted-ridge bathymetry; Cox–Doell–Dalrymple's 1963–64 reversal timescale; the Eltanin-19 profile, 1965–66) — but its promissory notes were honored at extraordinary speed: Wilson's 1965 transform-fault prediction was confirmed seismically by Sykes's 1967 focal-mechanism solutions, and Vine–Matthews 1963 was vindicated by DSDP Leg 3's 1968–69 sediment ages.
- **P5 — P:** The embedding was selective: isostasy (Airy, 1855) survives intact inside the new theory, and fixity is explained as the short-time limit (cm/yr motion is invisible to surveying, which is why a century of geodesy could sustain it) — but the geosyncline-contraction theory of mountains was refuted outright with no limiting case (the Appalachians became Iapetus closure, not a derivation from Hall and Dana).
- **P6 — P:** A genuine re-meaning occurred — the striped magnetic anomalies Mason and Raff had surveyed off the US West Coast (published 1961) sat uninterpreted until Vine and Matthews (1963) and the Eltanin profiles (1966) made the same geometry say "seafloor spreading taped the reversal history" — but the theory's core was a new kinematic ontology, not primarily a reinterpretation of a held formalism.
- **P7 — P:** The revolution's costs fell unevenly: its senior converts paid institutional coin — J. Tuzo Wilson, raised in Toronto's fixist school, had defended the settlement before producing transform faults (1965), and the Vine–Matthews paper endured two years of establishment silence — but the young founders (Vine, McKenzie, Morgan, Sykes) held little the theory asked them to surrender, unlike Newton's action-at-a-distance or Planck's continuity.
- **P8 — S:** The harvest continues to be un-ordered: the Wilson cycle, megathrust seismology (Tōhoku, 2011), ophiolite and terrane analysis, paleogeographic reconstruction and the supercontinent cycle (Rodinia to Pangaea), mineral exploration at plate boundaries, and the coupling of orogeny to long-term climate (Raymo and Ruddiman's uplift-weathering hypothesis, 1988) — sixty years and still generating.
- **P9 — S:** The theory closed the fifty-year drift crisis (Wegener 1912 through the 1926 symposium to 1968) and opened the deeper floor beneath its own success: the engine — the pattern and energetics of mantle convection (whole-mantle vs. layered; the plate-vs-plume controversy from Morgan 1971 onward) — plus the initiation problem (when and why plate tectonics began on Earth, and why Venus runs a stagnant lid instead), questions its successors still occupy.

## PART 3 — POINT NOTES

**P1 (Deletion).** "Made unthinkable" is a sociological test with a time lag, while "at birth" is logical. **T5 exposed this**: germ theory's birth (Pasteur 1857–64) killed spontaneous generation quickly, but the miasma deletion needed until roughly 1900 (Hamburg–Altona 1892) to finish — I coded S because both deletions occurred within the theory's career, but the point needs a *depth-of-the-deleted-object* criterion (deleting an entire causal modality vs. deleting an errant hypothesis) to avoid crediting every successful theory with deletion.

**P2 (Unification).** The exemplars are identity-claims (the apple and the Moon under *one* force; spacetime *one* thing), but outside mathematical physics theories deliver common-cause claims. **T5 exposed it**: I coded S only because germ theory dissolved the contagion-vs-miasma antinomy (one agent, both transmission modes — the 1851–83 Sanitary Conference deadlock), which is artifact-shaped; the surface reading ("a specific germ for each disease") is a *differentiation*, not a unification. **T3 exposed it from the other side**: the eccentric/epicycle equivalence is literally "a distinction that is an artifact of description," yet no one would call the *Almagest* profound — the point grades the *kind* of distinction collapsed, not its mere occurrence. **T14** added a third flavor (unification of *disciplines*: marine geophysics + continental geology), which the definition doesn't cover but felt like the real P2 event.

**P3 (Universal constant).** The point conflates determining a constant's *value* with *installing* it as universal. **T6 exposed this**: c's value was determined inside the ether program (Fizeau 1849; Weber–Kohlrausch 1856; Maxwell 1865), but its universality — the re-scaling of space and time — is Einstein's 1905 act; I coded P rather than F only because Maxwell's theory sits inside the panel's own scope line for T6. **T12** exposed the boundary problem: k_B entered only with Planck's December 1900 k = R/N, at the very edge of the theory's stated scope. And **T2/T5/T14** are structurally F regardless of depth — the point cannot discriminate profundity outside mathematical physics, which for this panel is a genuine design finding.

**P4 (Derivation-first).** The definition bundles two dissociable components: (a) old-data economy, (b) honored promissory notes. **T14 exposed this**: it fails (a) — the 1962–68 revolution was fed by the postwar oceanographic data flood — while satisfying (b) superlatively (Wilson 1965 → Sykes 1967; Vine–Matthews 1963 → DSDP Leg 3); **T5** similarly (Koch's experiments vs. Snow's old data). I weighted (a) because the definition's own sentences ("Newton needed almost no new observations") make it primary, but that weighting is a judgment call the instrument under-specifies.

**P5 (Conservative embedding).** Direction ambiguity: the exemplars code the *new* theory's treatment of *its* predecessor, but for the panel's dead frameworks (**T1, T6, T9, T13**) the only interesting P5 fact runs the other way (was it conservatively embedded by its successor?). I coded forward consistently, but the reverse facts (Joule's refutation of caloric; SR's deletion of the ether) kept pressing on the codes. Second softness: recovering a theory's *laws* in a limit vs. inheriting its *practices* under a new rationale — thermodynamics recovers caloric bookkeeping at W→0 (T12 = S) while germ theory kept the sewers (T5 = P); **the T5/T12 pair** is the exposing case, and the point doesn't say which kind of survival counts.

**P6 (Formalism and meaning).** The point is structurally inapplicable where there is no formalism: **T2 (Darwin) is F by default though among the panel's deepest entries**, and T4, T7, T9, T13 are likewise F on structure rather than merit — the cell conflates "the trait could not arise" with "the theory lacks the trait." **T14 exposed a second softness**: is re-meaning *data* (Mason–Raff's 1961 stripes → Vine–Matthews's 1963 tape-recorder) the same trait as re-meaning *formalism* (Lorentz → Einstein)? I hedged with P.

**P7 (Founders' resistance).** The point bundles two traits — coin *paid* (Newton, Planck) and coin *declined* (late Einstein) — and presupposes founders who held something the revolution requires surrendering. **T14 exposed the presupposition**: its founders (Vine, McKenzie, Morgan, Sykes) were young men holding nothing the theory asked them to give up; the coin was paid by the *previous* generation (the 1926 symposium's victims). **T5's** best evidence (Koch weakening his own 1884 postulates for cholera) is methodological housekeeping rather than metaphysical surrender. I coded both P; a stricter rubric would give F.

**P8 (Generative slope).** The compound code carried 8 of 14 theories — the panel is thick with productive-then-doomed frameworks (caloric, epicycles, ether, miasma, phlogiston, fixism ×2) plus Darwin-within-scope, which suggests the informative half of the point is doing most of the panel's discrimination. Two softnesses: (i) "paid more for each new result" has no metric — I judged by the growth of ad hoc structure (land bridges, contraction patches, Protean phlogiston), which is qualitative; (ii) a theory can degenerate on one front while generating on another (Darwin's heredity problem worsened while biogeography stayed generative), which a single code cannot express. **Most candidly: T2's code is decided by the scope clause** — exclude the modern synthesis (as instructed) and the eclipse of Darwinism terminates the arc at "S then D"; include it and the answer is S. A scope line is doing philosophical work here.

**P9 (Crisis-chaining).** "Opening a deeper one" needs distinguishing from leaving an unsolved defect. Newton's open mechanism arises from the theory's *success* structure (the law works; the mechanism is withheld — "hypotheses non fingo"); phlogiston's weight anomaly arises from its *failure* structure. I used that discrimination to separate S from P (**T9, T3, T1 got P; T8, T10, T11, T12, T14 got S**). **T9 is the exposing case**: phlogiston did hand successors the pneumatic program, but its standing question was a wound, not a floor — and the point as written does not force that distinction.
