# PART 1 — THE ANOMALY LEDGER (16 entries; 1-12 strain the LambdaCDM settlement, 13-16 strain the rival)

**1. The cluster virial discrepancy (the root).**
(b) Velocity dispersion of Coma galaxies ~1000 km/s against its light:
Zwicky 1933 (*Helv. Phys. Acta* 6, 110) inferred "dunkle Materie" at a
factor of order a few hundred in his distance calibration (today:
total-to-baryonic mass ~= 1/f_b ~= 6-7, since cluster baryon fractions
are ~0.15). Threads: Smith 1936 (*ApJ* 83, 23, Virgo); 1970s-80s X-ray
hydrostatics (Jones & Forman and others); strong-lensing arcs (Soucail
et al. 1988; Lynds & Petrosian 1989). Modern counter-thread worth
recording: Abell 520's lensing "dark core" coincident with the gas
(Mahdavi et al. 2007; Jee et al. 2012/2014) — a standing embarrassment
requiring line-of-sight structure. (c) **Missing entity** — Zwicky's
placeholder became Omega_c; the settlement states it fluently; the
residual strain is that the entity has never been isolated (see #11).
(d) Thickness 3: Omega_c h^2; per-cluster NFW (M_200, c); hydrostatic
mass-bias f_bias. (e) **Absorbed** (dynamically); entity pending.

**2. Flat spiral rotation curves.**
(b) Orbital speeds refuse to decline beyond the optical disk: Rubin &
Ford 1970 (*ApJ* 159, 379, M31 optical, to ~24 kpc); Roberts &
Whitehurst 1975 (*ApJ* 201, 327, 21 cm flat to 30 kpc); Bosma 1981 (25
galaxies); Begeman, Broeils & Sanders 1991 (*MNRAS* 249, 523);
consolidated in SPARC (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016, *AJ* 152, 157:
175 galaxies). Threads: optical, radio, and interferometric (THINGS)
kinematics agree. (c) **Missing entity** — the halo; the settlement's
most successful single absorption. (d) Thickness: 2 per system (halo
mass, concentration), spent ~10^4 times over. (e) **Absorbed**.

**3. The baryonic Tully-Fisher relation / radial acceleration relation
with one universal a0.**
(b) Measured: g_obs = nu(g_bar/a0) g_bar with a single a0 = 1.2 x
10^-10 m s^-2 and scatter 0.05 dex over ~5 decades, ~2700 points, 153
galaxies (McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert 2016, *PRL* 116, 181101); the
prediction test: full rotation curves predicted from baryons alone
(McGaugh 2012, *PRL* 108, 191301, gas-rich dwarfs, no free mass per
galaxy); BTFR anchor: Tully & Fisher 1977; baryonic McGaugh et al.
2000 (*ApJ* 533, L99). Second thread: the weak-lensing RAR (Brouwer et
al. 2017, *MNRAS* 466, 2102, GAMA/KiDS shear to ~1 Mpc) — same
relation, different observable. (c) **Unspeakable** — the settlement's
vocabulary (halos assembled by gravity from Gaussian initial conditions,
plus feedback) contains no acceleration-scale primitive, so the measured
law — dynamics slaved to baryons through one universal constant, in the
regime where the settlement itself attributes 80-95% of the force to
the dark sector — can be simulated but not stated: saying it requires
either a constant the framework lacks or a feedback "conspiracy,"
which is a narrative, not a sentence in the theory's language. (d)
Thickness 4-6, the subgrid industry calibrated to reproduce the form
(IllustrisTNG model, Pillepich et al. 2018, *MNRAS* 473, 4077: wind
velocity, mass loading, star-formation threshold, IMF, gas-return —
>=4 of ~10 calibrated parameters bear directly on this). (e)
**Contested** — simulations reproduce the curve (Keller & Wadsley 2017;
Ludlow et al. 2017); no mechanism derives the constant or its
normalization.

**4. a0 ~= cH0/2pi.**
(b) Measured: a0 = 1.2 x 10^-10 m s^-2 from galaxy kinematics (Milgrom
1983, *ApJ* 270, 365; Begeman et al. 1991: 1.21 ± 0.24 x 10^-10;
SPARC-era fits unchanged) versus cH0/2pi = 1.04 x 10^-10 from Planck
2018 LambdaCDM (H0 = 67.36) — agreement to ~15%, tightening to ~6% if
one uses SH0ES' H0. Threads: kinematic (rotation curves, many groups,
four decades) and cosmological (CMB+BAO) are fully independent
measurement domains. Possibly live at high z: Genzel et al. 2017
(*Nature* 543, 397, KMOS3D) find z~1-2 disks more baryon-dominated at
fixed acceleration, as an epoch-tied scale would suggest. (c)
**Unspeakable** — no LambdaCDM equation contains both symbols; the only
stateable sentence ("coincidence") is a refusal to speak, and the
settlement has no research program for it; Milgrom noted it in the
founding paper and mainstream reviews still omit it. (d) Thickness 0 —
no parameter has ever been spent. (e) **Open** (ignored footnote since
1983).

**5. Lambda's value and "why now."**
(b) Measured: late-time acceleration from two independent teams, Riess
et al. 1998 (*AJ* 116, 1009) and Perlmutter et al. 1999 (*ApJ* 517,
565); Omega_Lambda = 0.685 (Planck 2018). (c) **Missing constant** —
Lambda has been in the vocabulary since 1917, so the measurement is
speakable and absorbed; the residue (rho_Lambda ~ 10^-122 x naive
vacuum scale; Omega_Lambda ~= Omega_m only now — the coincidence
problem, Weinberg 1989, *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 61, 1) is unsayable but is
theory-side, not a measured contradiction. (d) Thickness 1 (Lambda);
dynamical-DE add-ons (w, w_a, c_s) tried and rejected. (e)
**Absorbed** (measurement) / **open** (value).

**6. Core-cusp and the dwarf diversity problem.**
(b) Measured: dwarf/LSB rotation curves imply constant-density cores
where NFW predicts rho ~ r^-1 (Navarro, Frenk & White 1996/97): Flores
& Primack 1994 (*ApJ* 427, L1); Moore 1994 (*Nature* 370, 629); de Blok
& McGaugh 1997; THINGS (Oh et al. 2011, *AJ* 142, 24). Second thread:
stellar kinematics of classical dSphs (Walker & Penarrubia 2011,
*ApJ* 742, 20 — cores in Fornax/Sculptor). Regularity thread: the
common ~10^7 solar masses within 300 pc across four decades of
luminosity (Strigari et al. 2008, *Nature* 454, 1096). Residual
strain: diversity — same stellar mass, inner velocities differing by 2x
(Oman et al. 2015, *MNRAS* 452, 3650). (c) **Missing constant** — no new
ontology is invoked; the absorption is calibration of known baryonic
physics (supernova-driven potential fluctuations: Pontzen &
Governato 2012). (d) Thickness 4: feedback coupling efficiency,
gas-fraction blowout threshold, star-formation density threshold,
reionization/photoheating amplitude. (e) **Contested** — cores are now
produced in simulations, but the diversity pattern and
feedback-budget limits in the smallest galaxies persist.

**7. Missing satellites and too-big-to-fail.**
(b) Measured (historically): CDM subhalo counts exceeded the ~11 known
Milky Way satellites by ~an order of magnitude (Klypin et al. 1999,
*ApJ* 522, 82; Moore et al. 1999, *ApJ* 524, L19); updated census 60+
(Koposov et al. and Bechtol et al. 2015, DES) with completeness-corrected
counts now matching LambdaCDM (Newton et al. 2018). TBTF: the most
massive subhalos are too dense to host the bright classical dwarfs
(Boylan-Kolchin, Bullock & Kaplinghat 2011, *MNRAS* 415, L40). (c)
**Missing constant** — reionization, feedback, and selection-function
calibration; all within vocabulary. (d) Thickness 4: reionization
epoch/intensity, small-halo star-formation efficiency, sky-coverage
incompleteness function, disk-heating amplitude (SIDM cross-section as
an alternative spare part). (e) **Absorbed, with residue** (edge cases
persist).

**8. Planes of satellites.**
(b) Measured: ~half of M31's satellites lie in a thin (~15 kpc),

62; claimed p ~ 10^-3 against LambdaCDM analogs); Milky Way VPOS

ontological. (d) Thickness 2 (infall statistics; LMC satellite

the M31 plane the least-absorbed.

**9. The H0 tension.**
(b) Measured: 73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc (SH0ES, Riess et al. 2022, *ApJL*
934, L7; first 3 sigma claim Riess et al. 2016, *ApJ* 826, 56) versus
67.36 ± 0.54 (Planck 2018 LambdaCDM, *A&A* 641, A6) — ~5 sigma.
Threads: TRGB/CCHP sits between (Freedman et al. 2019, *ApJ* 882, 34:
69.8 ± 1.9); time-delay lensing sides with SH0ES (H0LiCOW, Wong et al.
2020, *MNRAS* 498, 1420: 73.3 ± 1.8). (c) **Missing entity** (the live

**10. The S8 / cluster-normalization tension.**
(b) Measured: Planck SZ cluster counts give low sigma_8 normalization

**15. The relativist-completion bill: CMB peaks and c_gw = c.**

041501); the surviving construction (Skordis & Zlosnik 2019, *PRL*
123, 161103) fits the CMB and wave speed but through a bespoke
vector-tensor sector whose free functions are chosen to save the data.

function squeezed into the corridor between galaxy fits and
Cassini-class ephemeris limits (Hees et al. 2014). (c) **Missing
entity** (new fields at mounting cost), with the historical note that
the 2017 event was genuinely contradiction-class — the measured
equality was unsayable in the then-leading completion's vocabulary.
(d) Thickness >= 5 (free functions, couplings, shared 11 eV
neutrino). (e) **Contested** — no consensus parent theory exists.

for both programs this is calibration (inclination, EFE strength,
stripping conditions), not new ontology. (d) Thickness 1-2. (e)
**Contested**, possibly self-resolving.
